5. DESIGN DECISIONS
======================================================================
 • ONE engine: money_order_parts_for() gives each order's parts; the Sales
   profit engine, money_profit_parts, Reports and order_profit use it. The
   start date filter sits in that engine, so no screen has its own date.
 • Old Kaarbar bills are inside money_profit_parts (old_* lines, counted
   in net profit) so Home, Charts and P&L agree. They follow the start
   date; none count today. Not deleted.
 • The opening balance for orders placed before the start date and not
   delivered then (not taken from lots) = cost of their goods − what was
   paid before. An accountant would say: revenue is earned on delivery, so
   at the start date those goods were stock in transit, not a sale.
 • A missing cost counts ৳0 and is flagged (accountants call it
   "recognise the revenue, flag the cost"). Typing the price later lowers
   profit on the delivery day (or posts an adjustment if that month is
   locked).
 • Bills paid outside a payout are their OWN line, "Steadfast bills paid
   outside a payout", not inside "Courier (not billed yet)": Steadfast did
   bill them, they are just not paid from any of your accounts yet.
 • Courier lines are shown in "Investors and what I owe" under Other
   people (only when not zero), so that page still adds up to "To give".
 • Customer credit = money paid above each order's total (cancelled /
   returned orders ask nothing) + a previous balance paid beyond zero. Dues
   are NOT netted against credit (one order may owe while another is in
   credit) — the card shows both, and Batch "Deposit" can apply it.
 • The frozen expected fee is kept even if the order is reopened and
   delivered again ("never moves"); the real Steadfast bill always wins.
 • Locked months: a snapshot per order at lock time + an adjustment row
   for each later change. Unlocking clears the snapshot (the month then
   follows live numbers); adjustments already posted stay where posted.
 • "Balanced" = difference exactly ৳0.00 (to the paisa). The live data is
   ৳0.00 today, so no rounding slack was needed.
 • Deleting delivered orders: the existing rule already was "Super Admin +
   Safety Lock"; I kept it, made the staff refusal explicit, and added the
   Activity Log line. An order in a locked month stays refused by the lock.
 • "Show the sums" line "Orders from before Inventory mode (outside
   profit)" now reads "Old sales changed later, parcels with no order
   (outside profit)" — the old words would be wrong.
 • No new colour. The only orange on the order sheet is its Save button.
