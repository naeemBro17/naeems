WHAT WAS BUILT (short)
====================================================================
1. Give an offer (Inventory → Tools → "Give an offer (expiring stock)").
   For every lot row with units left and an expiry date:
   - speed = units sold in the last 90 days ÷ 3 (a month) — the SAME speed
     as the Stock report. A product with less than 30 days of history uses
     what exists (units so far scaled to 30 days) and is marked "new".
   - months to sell out = units ÷ speed. Units from lots that expire
     earlier sell first, so they are counted too (the card says so).
   - Needs more months than (months to expiry − 1) → "Give offer" (red).
     Within one month of that → "Watch" (amber). Otherwise not listed.
     No sale at all in 90 days → "Give offer".
   - Each card: product, lot, expiry, units left, speed, time to sell vs
     time to expiry, the price now, the LOWEST price (landed cost of a pack,
     rounded up to whole taka) and the suggestion: 5–10% off for Watch,
     10–20% for Give offer, as taka prices — never below the lowest price.
     If even the smallest discount would go below cost it says "At most N%
     off before it goes below cost"; if the price is already at cost, "No
     room".
   - The reason in one line, e.g. "Sells 2 a month, 14 left, expires in
     5 months (Mar 2027), needs about 7 months to sell."
   - "Open product" opens the product editor (to set an offer price).
   - "Already expired": units whose expiry month has ended, with their
     landed cost and a "Write off" button (the Batch 41 write-off, reason
     Expired, from that exact lot; type the number of units to confirm).
     It works only while Inventory mode is On, like Remove stock.

2. Buying list (Inventory → Tools → "Buying list (next trip)").
   - Pick Supplier or Country (from your lists), "Next stock arrives in
     about N weeks" (default 6) and "Keep stock for M months after it
     arrives" (default 3).
   - Products shown = the ones USUALLY bought there: the supplier / country
     of most of that product's purchase lots (a tie → the newest).
   - Suggested = speed × (N weeks + M months) − units left − units on the
     way, rounded UP to whole packs. "On the way" = lots marked "costs
     pending" with a date after today.
   - No sale in 120 days → "Skip — not moving" (0).
   - Each row: the reason, last landed cost, the suggestion, a box to change
     the number, and its money. At the top: estimated money needed in total
     (packs × newest landed cost) and how many products to buy.
   - "Download Excel" (product, packs, units, last buy price, landed cost,
     money needed, notes; your changed numbers, marked "Changed from 8").

3. Below cost on New order and Edit order.
   Under a line whose price (after that line's discount) is lower than the
   landed cost of the lot that sells next (earliest expiry first, the same
   order sales take stock in): "Below cost". You (and staff with "View
   profit & costs") see "Below cost — cost ৳X each, loss ৳Y". Staff without
   it see only "Below cost — check with Naeem" — the database does not even
   send them the taka. It is only a line of text: saving is never blocked.

4. Investor report PDF (Reports → Investors → "Investor report (PDF)").
   Pick the investor and a month (or use the report's dates) → one A4 page
   in English: NAEEM'S, the period, sales, net profit, return on money in
   the business %, that investor's capital put in / returned / still owed,
   profit share paid in the period, and the year by month (sales and net
   profit, with a total). Black text on white; the only colour is a small
   orange mark on the logo line. No product costs, no customer names.
   Buttons: Download PDF and Share (a phone opens the share sheet —
   WhatsApp etc.; a computer downloads it).
   A sample made by the tests: reports/batch-43-samples/investor-report-sample.pdf

====================================================================
CHOICES I MADE FOR YOU (and why)
====================================================================
1. "Time to expiry" runs to the END of the expiry month (Mar 2027 = good
   until 31 Mar), and a product is "expired" once that month has ended —
   the same as the Stock report and the Dashboard.
2. Units from lots that expire earlier count before a later lot (sales take
   the earliest expiry first), so a later lot can need an offer even if it
   is small.
3. Speed for products with 30–90 days of history is still ÷ 3, exactly as
   the brief and the Stock report say; under 30 days it uses what exists
   and says "new". So speeds never differ between screens.
4. Discount prices are rounded to whole taka; the lowest price is rounded
   UP, so a price at the floor is never a loss.
5. Below cost uses the line's own price (its discount included). An order
   discount on the whole order is not spread over the lines — it would
   change the warning every time the discount box changes. If a product has
   no units left in any lot, the newest landed cost is used; a product
   never bought gives no warning.
6. A product that only exists in the opening stock (L-000) has no supplier,
   so it is on no buying list until it is bought in a purchase lot once.
7. The buying list's money uses the newest LANDED cost (with shipping), not
   just the buy price, because that is the money the trip really needs; the
   Excel shows both.
8. The investor PDF uses the invoice's font (Plus Jakarta Sans) so ৳ and
   Bangladeshi number grouping (1,48,250) print correctly on every phone.
   The monthly table is the calendar year of the period's end, up to this
   month.
9. Give an offer and Buying list sit in Inventory → Tools (they are about
   stock); the investor PDF sits on the Investors report it comes from.

WHAT TO CHECK ON YOUR PHONE (step by step)
====================================================================
Nothing below changes your books if you follow it exactly.
 1. Admin → Inventory. Under TOOLS there are two new rows: "Give an offer
    (expiring stock)" and "Buying list (next trip)".
 2. Open Give an offer. Today it says Inventory mode is Off and shows
    nothing (you have no lots yet). Tap the chips All / Give offer / Watch
    / Expired; Back returns to Inventory.
 3. Open Buying list. Tap Supplier, then Country; the lists are your
    suppliers / countries (empty until you save a lot). Change the weeks to
    8 and back to 6.
 4. Orders → New order. Add any product. No "Below cost" line appears while
    you have no lots (there is no cost to compare with). Leave without
    saving.
 5. Reports → Investors. Scroll down to "Investor report (PDF)". If you
    have added an investor in Money, pick them and a month, tap Download
    PDF, open it: one page, NAEEM'S at the top. Tap Share: the share sheet
    opens (WhatsApp). Until you add an investor the card does not show.
 Later, once the opening stock is in and Inventory mode is On:
 6. Give an offer lists the expiring products with a reason; open one with
    "Open product" and set an offer price no lower than the "Lowest price".
 7. In a manual order, type a price under the cost on purpose: the red
    "Below cost — cost ৳X each, loss ৳Y" line appears; remove the line.
