1. WHAT I DID
------------------------------------------------------------

The side cart. A panel now slides in from the right side of the screen.
The FIRST time a customer adds something during a visit, it opens by
itself with a green tick and "Added to cart"; the product just added sits
at the top with a soft green highlight, and the panel gives a small
"nudge" so the eye notices it. After that first time, adds do not open
the panel again: a little copy of the product photo flies in an arc into
the floating cart bubble and the bubble's number pops. Tapping the bubble
opens the same panel titled "Your cart" with "2 items" beside it. In the
panel each line has its photo, name, option, price, a small "- 1 +"
stepper and "Remove" (which shows the usual "Removed / Undo" message).
Below: "Total (3 items)" and the amount, then "View cart", "Checkout"
(the one orange button) and a soft blue "Keep shopping". It closes with
Keep shopping, the X, a tap on the dimmed page, the phone's Back button,
Escape, a swipe to the right, or any page change. I used your approved
code and CSS exactly; the adaptations are listed in section 5.

Same numbers as the cart page. The panel does not have its own price
maths. It reads the cart lines, prices (including campaign sale prices),
stock limits and the total from the same code the cart page uses, so the
panel total and the cart page total are always the same (a test checks
this). "+" stops at the same limit with the same limit message. To make
sure they really share one source, I moved two pieces out of the cart
page into shared helpers that both now use: the "+ limit" rule
(src/hooks/useCartLineLimits.ts) and the Checkout button's steps
(src/features/checkout/useCartCheckout.tsx: record the start of
checkout, then go to /checkout/delivery if signed in, otherwise show the
Google sign-in sheet). The cart page works exactly as before. When the
panel opens it also re-checks live stock once, like the cart page does
when it opens, so a line that sold out meanwhile is corrected.

Where adds go through the new rule. Every customer "add" now calls the
same two-line rule (open the panel on the first add, otherwise fly): the
product page's Add to Cart and its "+", the product cards' cart button
and their "+" (this covers Home, Search, brand pages and "You may also
like"), the option picker sheet, and the Wishlist page. Haptic buzz and
tracking are unchanged. Admin screens do not use it.

The floating cart bubble. A tap now opens the side cart instead of going
to /cart. You can drag it with your finger: it follows the finger, and
when you let go it snaps to the nearest side (12 px from the edge,
inside the phone's safe area), never under the top bar, the Home tab bar
or the product page's buy bar. Its side and height are remembered on
the phone, for the next page and the next visit. Until it has been
moved, it keeps the old product-page behaviour (sits just above the buy
bar and moves with it); once moved, it stays where it was put and only
keeps clear of the buy bar. It is now a real button (it was a link).

The product page.
- The heart moved from the top bar onto the photo, bottom-right (where
  "1 / 5" used to be), 16 px in from the edge so a photo swipe does not
  hit it. The "1 / 5" counter moved to the bottom-left. The top bar has
  only Back and Share. The row of dots under the photo is gone.
- A full-width orange "Add to Cart" button now sits in the page right
  after the options (and before the wholesale price). Once the product
  is in the cart it becomes "-  1 in cart  +" with a near-black border.
  Sold out shows the same grey "Sold out". It runs exactly the same code
  as the buy bar.
- The buy bar at the bottom is hidden while that in-page button is on
  screen and slides in once it has scrolled away (it no longer reacts to
  scroll direction). The bubble still knows where the bar is.
- First screen: on 390 x 844 and 360 x 780 phones, without scrolling,
  you see the whole photo, brand, name, price, stock, delivery line,
  options and the in-page Add to Cart. The page measures this itself and
  only makes the photo shorter if things would not fit (never the text);
  the photo always stays whole.
- "About this product" shows 4 lines and then "Read more" ("Show less"
  folds it again), only when the text is longer than 4 lines. Text stays
  left-aligned. Everything else on the page is unchanged.

Kaarbar-style search. A new search (src/lib/smartSearch.ts, your exact
code) splits what you type into words; a product matches when every
word is the START of a word in the product, in any order. So "cerave 236
aus f" finds "CeraVe Foaming Cleanser 236ml (AUSTRALIA)" and not the
Hydrating (UK) one, "aus cerave" works too, and a typo like "cerve"
still finds CeraVe (close-spelling fallback). It is now used in:
- the shop search (name, brand, category, and the product's and its
  options' Region / Size). Recent searches, suggestions, the dropdown,
  tracking and the category filter are unchanged.
- Admin > Products list (name, brand, SKU). The list keeps your filters.
- Admin > New order and Edit order product search (name, option labels,
  brand, SKU). The same search also serves the campaign editor and the
  combo editor, which already used it.
- The lot form's product picker (name and SKU) and the Bento tile
  product picker (name and brand), which used a plain "contains" search.

Order Placed.
- The sound button left the header. It now sits in the scene's
  bottom-right corner just below the road line: a 44 px tap area with a
  36 px look, muted colour, above the drawing. A test taps it once at its

- Dates (option C): "Arriving between", then "28 Nov  -van-  29 Nov"
  with the weekday under each date and a little van on the line between.
  A one-day delivery shows a single date ("Arriving on") with no line.
  The words read by screen readers are unchanged.
- The four buttons are one 2 x 2 grid of equal buttons, 52 px high:
  Keep shopping (soft blue), View my order (the only orange), Invoice and
  Need help? (outlined). Without a Need help? link, Invoice spans both
  columns. At 360 px no text is cut or wrapped.
- Old CSS no longer used (b69-eta, b69-days, b69-day, b69-dash,
  b69-row1) was removed after checking nothing else uses it.

Admin tick text. Inventory > lot detail: "Allocated total = lot total"
with a tick character now reads "Allocated total matches the lot total".

Tests. New unit tests (src/lib/batch70.test.ts, 13 tests): the panel

Performance check (Chrome, CPU slowed down 4x, 390 x 844):
- Dragging the bubble and snapping: 101 frames, none slow, no long task.
- Swiping the panel closed: 83 frames, none slow, no long task.
- Opening the panel from the bubble: 46 frames, none slow, no long task.
- First add (panel opens with the nudge): one slow frame, longest task
  129 ms. The SAME single task (130 ms) happens on an add with the panel
  turned off, and a CPU profile shows the shop's own code takes only a
  few milliseconds of it; the rest is the browser's own style, layout
  and paint work after the cart changes (plus the test tool's click).
  So the panel and the fly add no jank. Dropped frames during the new
  movements: no.
- No new npm package. Every movement uses only transform and opacity.
  Finger-follows write the position straight to the element inside
  requestAnimationFrame; no React re-render per finger move.

Build: npm run build passes with zero errors. Unit tests: all 69 files

4. WHAT I FOUND ALONG THE WAY
------------------------------------------------------------

- My mistake, please read: for the performance check I first ran a
  small measuring script (not part of the test suite) that did NOT block
  the Facebook Pixel and Google Analytics the way the tests do. Those
  first runs sent a handful of fake events to your real Pixel and GA:
  page views, product views and about 3 "Add to cart" events, all from
  the address "localhost" on 27 Nov 2026. No order and no purchase event
  was sent. If you want your numbers clean, filter out hostname
  "localhost" in GA, and in Facebook Events Manager these show with the
  domain localhost. I blocked tracking for every later run and checked
  that nothing leaked.
- Desktop-mouse drag in Chrome's phone emulation sometimes cancels
  itself at 360 px (the browser fires "pointercancel"). A real finger
  does not do this (the bubble tells the browser not to scroll from it),
  and the tests drag with a real touch. If it ever happens, the bubble
  simply snaps to the side it is nearest, nothing breaks.
- One older test on Home (batch-29 "a logo that cannot be read still
  shows") failed once during the batch with "element is not attached"
  (the page re-drew the brand row while the test scrolled to it). It
  passed in the full run. This batch does not touch the brand row.
- "View my order" on Order Placed uses the site's --color-primary
  (#F05020), a redder orange than the brand orange #FF7A45 that Add to
  Cart uses. I used the brand
  orange for the side cart's Checkout (it matches your mockup and Add to
  Cart). The two oranges side by side are worth unifying one day.

5. DESIGN DECISIONS (adaptations to the real app)
------------------------------------------------------------

- Where the panel shows: on every page except the cart, checkout,
  account, orders, wallet and admin (your "never" list). The brief also
  says "the same pages as the bubble", but the Wishlist page has an Add
  button and no bubble; with only the bubble's pages, a first add on the
  Wishlist would have used up the "first add" without showing anything.
  So it shows on the Wishlist page too.
- Combo page "Add all to cart": NOT changed. It already takes the
  customer straight to the cart page, so opening a panel for a moment
  would only waste the "first add" of the visit. It keeps working as
  before.
- Option picker sheet: on the first add the sheet closes and the panel
  opens (the panel says "Added to cart", so the old "Added to cart:
  option" toast is skipped then). On later adds the photo flies and the
  toast still shows. The Wishlist page does the same with its toast.
- Phone Back: like every sheet in the shop, the open panel adds one
  same-page history step, so Back closes it. When it opens right after
  the option sheet closes, it waits for that sheet's own step to finish
  first, so the two never cross.
- Checkout when signed out: the panel closes first, then the sign-in
  sheet opens (the sign-in sheet sits lower than the panel on screen).
- While the panel is open, "Removed / Undo" and the limit message sit
  above it (Undo just above the panel's total).
- Colours: your CSS asked for var(--color-primary, #ff7a45) on Checkout;
  on this site --color-primary is #F05020, so I used --color-accent
  (#FF7A45), which is the brand orange the mockup shows. All other
  tokens (--color-surface, --color-border, --color-text-*) exist as
  named.
- Bubble: tap and drag on one element, so it became a <button> instead
  of a link. "Open in new tab" on the bubble no longer exists (it opens a
  panel now, not a page).
- In-page stepper wording: "1 in cart" between - and +, as in your brief.
- The photo height is measured by the page (the space from the photo's
  bottom down to the in-page Add to Cart) instead of a fixed CSS guess,
  so long names or options never push the button off the first screen.
  It is measured before the page is drawn, so nothing jumps.
- Buy bar when the in-page button is still BELOW a very short screen:
  the bar shows (so there is always a way to buy). On normal phones the
  in-page button is on the first screen, so the bar is hidden there.
- The shop search still treats a single letter as "no search yet" (as
  before), so the dropdown does not pop up for one letter.
- The admin Products list now ranks the best matches first while
  searching (like Kaarbar); with an empty search it keeps your order.

------------------------------------------------------------
6. INDUSTRY-LEVEL SUGGESTIONS
------------------------------------------------------------

1. One orange (small). Two oranges are in use (#FF7A45 and #F05020).
   Shoppers read colour as brand; two shades side by side look
   unfinished. Make --color-primary the brand orange or retire it.
2. Free-delivery nudge in the side cart (medium). The panel is now the
   place customers look at most after adding. A line such as "Add 350
   more for free delivery" there is a proven way to raise order size.
   Only if you offer such a threshold.
3. "You may also like" inside the side cart (medium). One small row of
   2-3 related products under the lines turns the moment of adding into
   a second sale. Keep it light (small photos only).
4. Show stock warnings in the panel (small). The cart page shows "Get it
   in 3 to 5 days" for lines bought after the order; the panel does not.
   Showing it there avoids surprises at checkout.
5. Search result highlighting (small). Bold the typed word parts in the
   search suggestions so shoppers see why a product matched.
6. Pixel and GA safety for scripts (small). The test suite blocks
   tracking, but any hand-run script can still send events (as happened
   in this batch). Turning tracking off on "localhost" inside the app
   itself would make this impossible.
