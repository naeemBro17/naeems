1. WHAT I DID
-------------

PART 1 — New shell: title, menu, groups
  - Computer (1024px and wider): a left sidebar exactly like the mockup,
    titled "NAEEM'S SUPER ADMIN", grouped:
      Home · Orders (orange count = orders waiting to confirm) · Customers
      CATALOG: Products · Categories · Import / Export
               (Brands has its place reserved in the code, hidden until
               Batch 26 builds it)
      SALES: Promo Codes · Wholesalers · Reviews
      STORE DESIGN: Bento Tiles · Banner & Texts
      ADMIN: Team · Activity Log · Settings
    "Signed in as <username>" sits at the foot of the sidebar.
  - Every page header: big title on the left; on the right the light/dark
    switch, "View site" (opens the shop in a new tab) and the page's one
    orange main button ("+ Add product", "+ New order", ...).
  - Phone and tablet (under 1024px): a bottom bar with exactly 5 tabs:
    Home · Orders (badge) · Products · Customers · More. "More" lists
    everything else in grouped rounded lists, with the small orange
    "Super Admin" pill on Team and Activity Log, exactly as in the mockup.
  - Everyone only sees what they are allowed. An empty group is hidden. If
    someone can't open Customers, the next item they may open takes its
    place in the bottom bar (so a 5-tab bar stays 5 tabs).
  - The open page and every filter are in the web address, so phone Back
    and refresh keep your place. Opening a menu item is a real step in
    history: Back takes you to the page before.
  - Staff see "NAEEM'S <ROLE NAME>" (e.g. NAEEM'S MANAGER) instead.

PART 2 — Home (the dashboard)
  - "Good morning / Good afternoon / Good evening, <name>" in Bangladesh
    time, under the small orange "NAEEM'S SUPER ADMIN".
  - 4 cards: Today's orders (count + ৳, the orange-tinted card), To confirm,
    On the way (booked on Steadfast, not yet delivered), This month (৳ +
    order count). Cancelled orders never count. Tapping a card opens the
    Orders list already filtered.
  - NEEDS ATTENTION: orders waiting to confirm, products low on stock,
    reviews waiting for approval, wholesalers waiting for approval. Each row
    shows its count and opens that list filtered. Rows with 0 are hidden;
    when everything is 0 it says "All caught up. Nothing is waiting for you."
  - All numbers come from the database in ONE call (admin_dashboard). The
    database itself decides what each person gets: money only for you or
    for staff with the new "See sales figures" switch (off by default);
    each count only if they may open that section.
  - Money uses Bangladeshi grouping: ৳1,24,300.
  - Low-stock threshold: Settings → Orders → Low-stock alert (default 5).

PART 6 — Roles
  - Team now has two tabs: Staff and Roles.
  - Roles: "Create role" (a name and the same switches as Batch 24, plus
    "View wholesalers" and "See sales figures"). Tap a role to rename it or
    change its switches. "Delete role" only works when nobody has the role
    (the database refuses otherwise).
  - Adding or editing a staff member: pick their role. Their permissions
    come from the role. Changing a role's switches changes everyone with
    that role at once — the database reads the role on every single action
    (staff_can()), even for someone already signed in.
  - Existing moderators: you had none on the live site, so no role was
    needed. (The migration would have made one role per distinct set of
    switches, the first named "Moderator", keeping everyone's exact
    permissions.)
  - Role created / changed / deleted and "role given to X" are in the
    Activity Log.
  - Proof nothing got looser: the e2e tests sign in as a limited-role
    staff member and call the database directly: customers, wholesalers,
    roles, role changes, status changes, Activity Log, and money on Home are
    all refused or empty. A role change switches Customers on and off
    straight away, without signing in again.

5. DESIGN DECISIONS
-------------------
  - Mockup followed exactly for the 4 screens (phone Products, More, Home;
    computer Products). Small differences, and why:
      * The phone search box has a magnifier on the left (the mockup has
        none) and a slider icon on the right instead of the gear: it opens
        the filters + Select.
      * Computer Products has a 4th dropdown "Live: All / Live / Hidden",
        so the old Active/Inactive filter isn't lost.
      * More has an extra row "View site" and "Signed in as ..." at the
        bottom (the phone had no other way back to the shop).
      * Search boxes are 44px tall on a phone (the mockup's are ~38px),
        for the 44px tap rule.
      * The Wholesalers badge is no longer in the menu (the mockup shows
        only the Orders badge); pending wholesalers show on Home instead.
  - Orange: the mockup itself uses it for the title label, Today's card,
    the To-confirm number, attention counts and the active tab. Other pages
    use it once (the main button). Approve buttons on reviews are grey now.
  - Staff with no role yet (only possible from before roles) keep their
    own switches until you pick a role for them.
  - Sign out (Settings → Other) is a grey button, not red: it isn't
    destructive.
  - Settings and Banner & Texts open each setting as its own page, so a
    phone never has to scroll through 8 forms.

DESIGN IDEAS (NOT BUILT) — pick any for a later batch
  - Order detail as a full page on a computer (two columns: items left,

HOW TO CREATE A ROLE AND ADD A STAFF MEMBER
------------------------------------------
1. Admin → Team (phone: More → Team) → "Roles" tab → "Create role".
2. Type a name (e.g. "Manager"), switch on what they may do → "Create role".
3. "Staff" tab → "Add staff member" → username, full name, phone,
   password, pick the role → "Add staff member".
4. Give them the username and password. They sign in at /admin-access →
   "Staff login". To change what all Managers can do later, edit the role.
