WHAT WAS BUILT (short)
====================================================================
A new admin section "Money" (in the menu after Steadfast payouts). Only you
(View profit & costs) can see it.

- Accounts: Cash, bKash, Bank (rename them or add more, e.g. Nagad), plus
  "With Steadfast", which you never type into: the COD money Steadfast
  collects goes in, and each payout goes out.
- Add money: one form. Big buttons at the top: Expense · Move money ·
  Investor · Me · Not shop money · Pay what I owe. Amount, account, date
  (today), note, Save. The form stays on the same kind and account after
  saving, so the next entry is quick.
- Expenses: a list by month and category, the total at the top and each
  category's share. You can add a bill photo; it is made small (under
  150 KB) on your phone before it is saved.
- Investors and what I owe: each investor's capital in, returned, still
  owed and profit share (this month, this year, all time); what you owe each
  supplier; other people you owe.
- Lots: "Paid for this lot" (account + amount + date, one or more rows) on
  the lot form and the lot page. Whatever is not paid is owed to the lot's
  supplier.
- Customers: a "To receive" filter (largest due first, the total at the
  top). On a customer: their due, "Send due reminder" (opens WhatsApp with
  your message and a link to their own order page on your site), "Copy
  message", and the list of reminders already sent.
- Remove stock (on a product) and "Damaged — do not put back in stock" (in
  Mark returned). Both work only while Inventory mode is On.
- Opening balances: one screen you use once, on your start date.
- The Money page: Your profit (every line shown), the return on money in
  the business, Money now, and a balance check that says "Books balance" in
  green, or shows a red line with the difference.

PART 0 — WHAT ALREADY EXISTED (no changes made there)
====================================================================
- Customer payments live in ONE table, order_payments (Batch 30/32). Each
  row is a payment or a refund, with a method: bKash, Nagad, Cash, Bank,
  "COD via Steadfast", or Other. It also says where it came from: typed by
  you, "Mark fully paid", the old "paid" switch, or Steadfast. There is no
  separate "advance" method: a bKash advance is simply a bKash payment made
  before delivery.
- A COD payment collected by Steadfast is recorded automatically, once per
  order, as "COD via Steadfast" when Steadfast says the parcel was
  delivered.
- Batch 36 stores, for Steadfast:
  * the current balance (what Steadfast holds for you; today ৳0);
  * every payout: date, amount and the parcels in it (31 payouts so far,
    ৳1,51,058 in total);
  * for each parcel: COD collected, delivery charge, 1% COD fee, anything
    else kept, net paid;
  * for each order: what the courier kept (order_courier_costs). Batch 39's
    order profit already uses this.
  * It did NOT store which account a payout landed in. That is new now.
- Customer dues are worked out on the fly: for every order, the total minus
  what was paid (a cancelled order owes nothing). They are added up per
  customer: by their account, or by phone number when they have none.
- Batch 39 profit: order_profit (one order) and inventory_sales_profit
  (dates: sales, cost of goods, delivery, courier + COD fee, cost
  adjustments). The Money page builds on inventory_sales_profit. Nothing
  was copied.

CHOICES I MADE FOR YOU (and why)
====================================================================
1. Suppliers: I did not make a second supplier list. The lots already use
   the Batch 39 suppliers list, so "To give" reads suppliers from there. A
   new small list (parties_payable) holds only investors and "other people"
   you owe.
2. The books start at the BEGINNING of your start date: what you count is
   "as of the morning". Payments, payouts and entries dated on the start
   date or later count; everything before is in the opening balances. After
   you confirm, an entry dated before the start date is refused.
3. Paying for a lot is its own kind of entry ("Paid for a lot"). Paying the
   rest later is "Pay what I owe" (a supplier payment). Neither is an
   expense.
4. A payout that has arrived but is not placed yet stays counted in "With
   Steadfast". The Money page lists it with a Place button and offers the
   last account you used. So the books stay balanced until you place it.
5. A customer payment with a method no account takes ("Other", or Nagad
   before you add a Nagad account) goes to Cash. You can change which
   account takes which payments in Accounts.
6. A count difference (end-of-day count) is shown as its own line in the
   balance check. It is not inside Net profit: your profit formula is fixed
   and has no such line.
7. Money that is not the shop's (your consultancy) is the same: its own
   line in the balance check, never in profit.
8. A staff member with only "Add money entries" can add an expense or move
   money, nothing else. They see account names and categories (needed for
   the form), but no balance, list or profit.
9. Deleting an entry asks you to type the amount (Careful actions). Opening
   balances can only be removed by "Undo the opening", which needs the
   Safety Lock.
10. "Use the Kaarbar due" means Kaarbar's number is the whole due. The site
    orders' own due is taken off it, so nothing is counted twice. "Add both"
    adds the whole Kaarbar amount on top. "Keep the site due" adds nothing.
11. The balance check, in plain words:
    - What the business has: accounts + With Steadfast + what customers owe
      + previous balances + stock at landed cost + goods packed for orders
      not yet delivered.
    - Minus what it owes: To give + customers' advance payments + the price
      of orders not yet delivered (they become a sale only when delivered)
      + "sold without stock" costs still to come.
    - This must equal: opening net worth + net profit − investor profit
      share − your withdrawals + your money put in ± not-shop money ± count
      differences ± changes on old orders from before Inventory mode (shown
      as their own line, because those orders are left out of profit as in
      Batch 39).
    - "Show the sums" lists every line.
    - Deleting a whole entry (with its money) keeps the books balanced. The
      check catches money or stock that moved without its matching record.
12. "The date it first went wrong" is the first day the Money page found
    the books off by more than ৳1 (one result is kept per day). A wrong
    entry can't be traced back to a day it was never looked at.
13. Return on money in the business = net profit ÷ the average of the money
    in the business at the start and at the end of the period.
14. The reminder link opens the customer's own order page on your site
    (Batch 35). Only a customer who has an account and is signed in can open
    it; for others it shows the sign-in. Customers who ordered through you
    by phone may not have an account.
15. Write-offs use units (pcs / ml / g), like lots. A damaged return is
    written off at the cost the sale took from the lot.

====================================================================
TO START USING INVENTORY (checklist)
====================================================================
 1. Pick your start date (for example the 1st of next month) and count all
    stock on that morning.
 2. Fill the opening stock file (Inventory → Opening stock → Download
    template) and upload it, dated the start date. Check the preview, then
    Confirm (this makes L-000).
 3. Fill the opening dues file from Kaarbar (Money → Opening balances →
    Download template). Upload it, check the totals against Kaarbar's
    "To Receive", and choose for each "already has a due" row.
 4. Type Cash, bKash and Bank as counted on the start date, and add "What I
    owe" (investors, suppliers) — match the total with Kaarbar's "To Give".
 5. Confirm the opening balances (once).
 6. Turn Inventory mode On (Inventory → Turn on Inventory mode → check
    screen → your password).
 7. From then on, add expenses, lot payments, investor money and
    withdrawals on Add money, and place each Steadfast payout. Look at the
    Money page: it should say "Books balance".
