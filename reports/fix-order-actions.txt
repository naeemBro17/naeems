FIX — ORDER PAGE ACTIONS + INVOICE FOLD LINE (version 1.38.1)
Branch: fix/order-actions-invoice (not merged, main not touched)

=====================================================================
1. WHAT I DID
=====================================================================

Order page (Admin → open an order)
- The "Other actions" card is gone. Invoice, Mark Delivered and Cancel
  no longer sit side by side.
- New "Invoice" card right under the status/amount card. It shows
  "INV-NM-xxxx · A4 · last printed <date time>" (or "Not printed yet")
  and three buttons: Print, Download, Share. These only make the PDF;
  they can never change the order. Share opens the phone's share sheet
  (WhatsApp etc.); where the browser can't share files, it downloads
  the PDF and says so. It uses the Batch 37 invoice and still marks
  the invoice as printed.
- New red "Careful actions" card at the very bottom (after History).
  One row per action with a one-line explanation:
  Mark in transit (other courier)…, Mark delivered…, Cancel order…,
  Delete order…. Only the actions allowed for the current status show.
- Nothing happens on one tap. Each opens a bottom sheet:
  * Cancel: "Cancel NM-xxxx?", what really happens ("N items go back to
    stock. The customer's due of ৳X is removed." and, if money was
    already recorded, that it stays recorded), a yellow warning if the
    parcel is booked with Steadfast, and the red Cancel button only
    turns on after you type the order number's digits (e.g. 2851).
  * Mark delivered / Mark in transit: a simple Keep / Confirm sheet.
  * Delete: you type the order number's digits before Delete turns on.
- Reopen: a cancelled order shows "Cancelled <date time> by <name>" and
  an orange "Reopen order" button (asks first). It puts the order back
  to the status it had just before the cancel and takes the stock
  again exactly like a new order. If any item is short it refuses with
  "Only N in stock for <product>." and nothing changes. It is written
  in the order history ("Reopened (stock taken again)") and in the
  Activity Log ("Status cancelled → confirmed").

Delete rule (restored and widened as asked)
- Orders NOT booked with Steadfast and NOT sent (Processing, Confirmed,
  Cancelled before booking) can be deleted after typing the number, by
  the same people as before (you, and moderators with "Delete early
  orders"). No Safety Lock needed. Stock goes back exactly as the old
  delete did.
- Booked, in transit and delivered orders still need the Safety Lock
  "Allow deleting orders at any stage".
- Orders list: the tick boxes + "Delete selected" are back for these
  orders (more orders now qualify). One order: type its number;
  several: type DELETE.
- What I restored, from git history: delete has used the same database
  function since Batch 24 (admin_delete_orders). Batch 35 moved the
  order into a full page and put Delete inside "Other actions"; the
  rule itself was "Pending, or Cancelled never booked" — so every
  Confirmed order needed the Safety Lock. I kept the Batch 24 function
  and its stock handling exactly, and only changed the rule line to
  "not booked AND (Processing, Confirmed or Cancelled)".

Invoice PDF
- Only ONE dashed fold line now, at exactly 1/3 of the page, under the
  label. The 2/3 line is gone. No text on or near it.
- The inside part (Sold by / Billed to / Invoice) starts with a clear
  gap below the line.
- Items table, totals, community box flow one after another; only the
  footer stays at the bottom of the last page.
- Long orders (more than 8 items): tighter rows and a small community
  box (QR 58 pt + "Join the NAEEM'S skincare community" + one line)
  under "Amount in words", left of the totals. 12 and 14 items fit on
  page 1; 20 items go to page 2 as before.

=====================================================================

I checked the live database. NM-2851 is NOT cancelled. It is still
"In transit", stock still taken, booked with Steadfast (306884413).
Its history has no cancel at all. What most likely happened: the old

  (Vitest), not in the e2e file: the PDF code needs Vite to load. The

=====================================================================
6. DESIGN DECISIONS
=====================================================================

- One orange per screen: Print is orange only when the page has no
  other main button (e.g. not on a Processing order, where "Confirm
  order" is orange, and not on a cancelled order, where Reopen is).
- "Mark in transit (other courier)" moved into Careful actions too (it
  also changes the order), with a simple confirm.
- If the PDF takes a moment to make, some phones refuse the share
  sheet; the card then says "The PDF is ready. Tap Share again."

=====================================================================
7. WHAT TO CHECK ON YOUR PHONE
=====================================================================

1. Open any order: Invoice card near the top, Careful actions at the
   bottom. Tap Print / Download / Share — the order never changes.
2. Tap "Cancel…" on a Confirmed order: the red button stays off until
   you type the number. Tap "Keep order".
3. Open a Confirmed order not yet booked → Delete… → type the number →
   it's deleted without the Safety Lock.
4. Print a long order (9+ items): one dashed line only, small QR box
   beside the totals.

=====================================================================
8. INDUSTRY-LEVEL SUGGESTIONS
=====================================================================

- Cancelling a booked parcel should also cancel it at Steadfast (their
  API supports a cancel request) — today it must be done twice; a
  missed one means a parcel goes out for an order you think is dead.
  Medium.
- Refunds on cancel: payments stay recorded after a cancel; a "refund
  needed" flag on cancelled orders with money taken would stop it
  being forgotten. Small.
