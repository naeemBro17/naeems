------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT I DID
------------------------------------------------------------

The new scene. On the Order Placed page, the old serum-drop animation
(from Batch 48) is gone. In its place is the small scene you approved: your
NAEEM'S shop on the left, the customer's home on the right, and the van
parked in front of the shop. The shop door opens, a parcel with your logo
comes out and hops into the van, the van dips under the weight, the door
shuts, and the van drives off toward the home with a puff of dust. The
home's window then lights up warm, and "Thank you, NAME!" and the rest of
the page rise in as before. I used your approved code exactly as given, with
no redesign, recolouring or re-timing. It lives in two new files:
src/components/checkout/PlacedScene.tsx (the drawing) and
src/styles/batch69.css (its colours and movement).

The van never stops. While the page stays open after placing the order, the
wheels keep turning, the road's lane marks keep sliding past and the van
bobs gently. This only happens on the FIRST arrival straight after "Place
order". If the customer reloads the page, or their phone is set to "reduce
motion", they see the still final picture at once: van halfway along the
road, window lit, nothing moving.

Phone buzz. On Android phones the phone now buzzes twice: a short buzz when
the parcel lands in the van, and a double buzz as the van drives off. As
before, this never happens on a reload or with "reduce motion", and a phone
that refuses vibration just shows the same screen.

Everything else on the page is unchanged: all texts, the summary card, View
my order, Invoice, Need help?, Continue shopping, the invoice download, the
purchase tracking, and the "plays only once" rule. There is no new text for
customers.

Clean-up. I deleted the old drop's code from the page and its styles from
src/styles/checkoutFinish.css (pipette, drop, splash droplets, rings, green
circle and tick). Before deleting each piece I searched the whole project to
make sure nothing else used it.

Tests. The automatic test for this page (e2e/batch-48.spec.ts) used to
check the drop. It now checks the van scene instead: picture frames at
0.3s, 0.7s, 1.15s, 1.9s, 2.6s and 3.4s; that only "move" and "fade" are
animated (the smooth, phone-friendly kind); that the van keeps driving;
the two buzzes; that a reload shows the still picture with nothing looping;
and that "reduce motion" shows the still picture. I added three new checks
that open the finished page at 390 px (light and dark) and 360 px (light),
and confirm: no sideways scrolling, the name NAEEM'S on the van ends before
the van's window, and the van does not overlap the home.

Checks run: npm run verify:quick, which is the build, all 916 unit tests,
and the 11 browser tests in e2e/batch-48.spec.ts. Result: ALL PASSED
(QUICK GREEN). As instructed, I did NOT run the full "npm run verify" (to
protect your Supabase bandwidth).

I looked at every picture myself. They are in reports/batch-69-screens/:
  01 to 06  the scene frame by frame (360 px)
  07-final-390-light, 07-final-390-dark, 07-final-360-light  the whole page

------------------------------------------------------------
6. INDUSTRY-LEVEL SUGGESTIONS
------------------------------------------------------------

1. Order confirmation by SMS (medium). Many shoppers in Bangladesh trust an
   SMS more than a web page. A short SMS with the order number right after
   "Place order" is a strong trust signal and cuts "did my order go
   through?" WhatsApp messages.
2. Battery and data on slow phones (small). The van keeps animating while
   the page is open. It only uses the cheapest kind of movement and costs
   no data, but a later batch could stop the loop when the page is in the
   background or after a minute, to be kind to older phones' batteries.
   (You asked for it to never stop, so I did not do this.)
