PART 2: BACK FROM A PRODUCT LANDS 4 PX SHORT
Found and fixed. The cause was not the scroll code: it was the four
section titles on the product page (Description, How to use and so on).
Their text height followed the font's own measurements, so each title was
1 px taller while the phone still showed the fallback font, and 1 px
shorter once the shop's real font finished loading. Four titles, 4 px:
the product page got 4 px shorter about a second after it opened. When
you were scrolled near the bottom of a product page (for example at "You
may also like"), the browser had to pull the page up by those 4 px, so
the spot that Back remembered was 4 px off. On a real phone the font
arrives later than on this computer, so it shows more often there.
Measured: the page was 1645 px tall, then 1641 px; after the fix it is
1641 px from the first frame and never changes. The titles now have a
fixed line height of 19 px, exactly the height they already had with the
real font, so nothing looks different. Only those titles changed; the
scroll code and every other page are untouched (forward taps still open
pages at the top).
How it was found: the full test run failed an older test (Back from one
product to another, "You may also like") by exactly 4 px, 802 instead
of 806, twice in a row. Measuring every element before and after showed
only the four titles changing. With the fix that test passes 3 out of 3
runs alone.
On the shop grid itself I could not make a gap appear: I measured phone
Back, the in-app Back arrow, a 6 times slower processor and real finger
scrolling, and the grid came back to the exact pixel every time. If you
still see a gap on the grid after this update, tell me your phone model
and browser and the next session can test exactly that.
New test (e2e/batch-64.spec.ts): shop at 390 px, scroll to a known card,
open it, phone Back and in-app Back: scroll position and the card's place
on screen must be within 1 px (the older tests allowed 5 px, which is why
4 px never failed before). It still respects reduced motion (the scroll
code is unchanged).

PART 4: "YOUR WALLET IS OPEN"
When you open a customer's wallet, the next time she opens the Account
page (or Home) she sees a floating card at the bottom: a small jar
drawing, "Your wallet is open", "You can now save with us for the products
you love. Add any amount and use it on your next order.", an orange "Open
my wallet" button (goes to her wallet) and a grey "Later". It fades and
slides up in, and fades out on Later (a plain fade for people who turn off
animations). Either button means she never sees it again on that phone.
 • It uses only what the app already knows: on Account, the wallet the
   page already loads; on Home, the copy of her wallet the app already
   keeps on her phone from the last Account / Wallet visit. Home never asks
   the database for anything new. That means: if she goes straight to
   Home after you open her wallet, she sees the note the first time she
   opens Account (or Home after that).
 • Never shown to visitors who are not signed in, to staff accounts, or to
   customers whose wallet is closed. If her phone blocks storage, it is
   never shown (better than showing it every time).
 • It is on top of the page, not inside it, so the Account page's order
   (header, wallet card, delivery details) is unchanged.
 • No new table, column or SQL.

PART 5: STEADFAST PAYOUTS STUCK ON "PROCESSING"
Found exactly as described: once a payout's parcel lines were saved, later
syncs only marked it "seen again" and never saved the new status. Now, when
a payout is skipped (nothing to read again), the status from Steadfast's
list is saved too if it changed. It is part of the same single update the
sync already made, so no extra call to Steadfast and no extra cost.
Nothing else in the sync changed (matching, money, courier costs).
After you deploy the two functions (section 2), the next sync (the 3-hour
refresh, or Refresh on the payouts screen) fixes the 4 stuck payouts by
itself.

PART 6: NEW ORDER, KEYBOARD AND A SAVED DRAFT
6a. In the customer list and the product list (New order, and the product
list on Edit order too): Arrow Down / Up move a highlight (stopping at the
first and last row), Enter picks it (a product with sizes opens the size
sheet, as a tap does), Escape closes the list as before, and typing puts
the highlight back on the first row. The highlighted row looks like a
hovered row plus a ring. The ring shows only when you use the keyboard,
so on the phone, taps and clicks look exactly as they did. Screen readers
hear the highlighted row. The combo editor is unchanged.
6b. While you type a New order, the form is kept on your device about
once a second (and the moment the page is hidden): customer, address,
items (with any price you typed), source, delivery, fee, discount, paid
now, admin note. Smart paste text is not kept. Each staff login has its
own draft; it is deleted after 12 hours, when the order is created, and
when you leave with "Discard changes? → Discard".
When you open New order and a draft exists, nothing is filled by itself.
A bar at the top says "You have an unsaved order from 10:42 PM." (with the
date too when it is not from today) with Restore and Discard. Restore
fills everything back exactly; prices and stock are still checked the
normal way when you tap Create order, and any product that no longer
exists or is hidden is left out with "1 item was left out because it is no
longer available." If your browser blocks storage, New order works
exactly as before, just without a draft.

======================================================================
5. DESIGN DECISIONS
======================================================================
 • Page titles: "Page | NAEEM'S" (straight bar) instead of a comma or
   colon. It is how most shops write browser-tab and link-preview titles.
 • Wallet note: the same floating glass card as the Pre-orders sheet (12 px
   from the edges, rounded corners), above the bottom nav on Home. No dim
   background: it is a note, not a question, so the page stays usable.
 • New order highlight ring: shown only in keyboard mode, so a phone tap
   never leaves a ring on the first row.
 • Draft while the Restore / Discard bar is open: nothing new is saved
   until you choose (so a fresh empty form can never overwrite the older
   draft by accident).
 • Restore keeps the prices you typed, but list prices, photos and stock
   come from today's products.

======================================================================
6. INDUSTRY-LEVEL SUGGESTIONS
======================================================================
 1. Fonts that change the page's size (small). The 4 px bug came from
    text whose height depends on the font. Giving the shop's fonts
    matching fallback sizes (a CSS size-adjust on the fallback) would stop
    this whole class of small jumps everywhere, also on first load.
 2. Wallet note across devices (small to medium). "Seen" lives on her
    phone, so on a second phone she sees the note once more. A
    "wallet_note_seen_at" column would make it once per customer, if that
    matters to you.
 3. Drafts for Edit order (small). Edit order has the same risk of losing
    typing on a reload; the same draft code could cover it.
 4. Payout dates (small). See section 4: refresh paid_at together with the
    status for already-synced payouts.
