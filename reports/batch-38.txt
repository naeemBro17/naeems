====================================================================
WHAT WAS BUILT (short)
====================================================================
- A new admin section "Inventory" (under Catalog). Only people with
  "View profit & costs" see it (today: only you, the Super Admin).
- Lots: each shipment or buying trip is one lot (L-001, L-002 ...). You
  enter the products, pieces, buy price (in any of 8 currencies), expiry
  month and weight, plus the bills: weight cost (luggage / cargo) and
  other costs (customs, courier ...). The shop works out the landed cost
  of every piece: buy price + its share of the weight cost (by grams) +
  its share of the other costs (by buy price). Shares add up to the lot's
  bills exactly, to the paisa.
- Weight of every product, guessed from the size in its name
  (473 ml ≈ 544 g). 90 of your 113 products and all 10 options got a
  weight automatically. 23 products have no size in the name; they are in
  "Needs weight", where you can type the weight.
- Opening stock: download an Excel template (one row per product / size),
  fill it, upload it, check the preview, then Confirm. This makes the one
  opening lot L-000.
- "Inventory mode" switch in Settings. It is Off and cannot be turned on
  yet ("Available after Batch 39 (sales use lots)"). The database also
  refuses it.

====================================================================
PART 0 — WHERE THINGS LIVE TODAY (no changes made here)
====================================================================
- Stock lives at BOTH levels:
  * A product with no options keeps its stock on the product
    (products.stock_quantity, plus an in/out-of-stock status).
  * A product with options (sizes / regions) keeps stock on each option
    (product_variants.stock_quantity, plus an in-stock flag).
  * Empty stock number = "not counted" (only in / out of stock).
  Today: 113 products, 5 of them have options (10 options in total);
  21 products have a counted stock number.
  So lots are built the same way: a lot row is a product, or a product +
  option.
- Checkout checks and reduces stock inside the database function
  place_order() (it re-reads stock and refuses "Only N left"). Manual
  orders use admin_create_manual_order(); cancelling gives stock back
  (cancel_order and the admin status change). None of these were changed.
- Permissions: staff_can('view_profit_costs') is the one server check.
  It is true for the Super Admin (is_admin()) and for an active staff
  member whose role has "View profit & costs". The website uses can(...)
  from the login (AuthContext) only to hide menus; the database checks again.
- Settings: Admin → Settings (app_settings table; only the Super Admin can
  change it). Activity Log: activity_log table, written by write_activity()
  and by triggers on products / options / orders. Safety Lock:
  Settings → Safety Locks; it needs your password and switches itself
  back on after 15 minutes (admin_safety_locks, admin_delete_lock_until()).

====================================================================
CHOICES I MADE FOR YOU (and why)
====================================================================
1. Exact money maths. All sharing is done in whole paisa with the
   "largest remainder" method: every row first gets its whole paisa, and
   the few paisa left over go one each to the rows with the biggest
   leftover fraction. Result: the shares always add up to the bill
   exactly. The cost PER PIECE can have more than 2 decimals (for example
   ৳211.055 when ৳422.11 is shared by 2 pieces); the row total is exact.
2. If every buy price in a lot is 0 (free samples), other costs are
   shared by pieces instead (you can't share by value when value is 0).
3. Lot numbers: one more than the highest lot number (L-001, L-002 ...).
   A deleted lot's number can be used again only if it was the last one.
   The opening lot is always L-000.
4. Editing a lot never rewrites history: if you change pieces from 3 to 5,
   the ledger gets a new row "+2 (Lot edited: pieces 3 → 5)".
5. A product that is in a lot can no longer be DELETED (hide it instead).
   Its stock history must stay. Products not in any lot delete as before.
6. Weight: an option uses its own size first, then its product's name.
   A typed weight is marked "manual" and the estimate never changes it.
   If you rename a product, an estimated weight is worked out again.
   Weights are not secret costs, so staff with "Edit products" may also
   type a weight. Weight edits show in the Activity Log as product edits
   ("weight_grams").
7. Opening stock file:
   - "source" is pre-filled "import" on every row; change it to
     "wholesale" where needed (most of your goods are imports).
   - Rows with no price AND no pieces are skipped (products you don't
     have). A row with only one of them is "must fix".
   - Duplicate rows (same product + same expiry month + same source) are
     merged: pieces added, buy price = average weighted by pieces.
     Import and wholesale rows of the same product stay separate, because
     only imports get the weight %.
   - Expiry can be typed 2027-03, 03/2027 or as an Excel date; it is
     stored as the last day of that month.
   - The product_key column is grey and marked "do not change", but the
     sheet is not password-locked: a locked sheet would stop you copying a
     row to split two expiry dates. A changed key is caught as
     "Unknown product_key".
   - The upload itself is written to the Activity Log (file name, rows,
     how many to fix), so you can see each try.
8. Excel library: two small, well-known libraries (read-excel-file and
   write-excel-file). They load only when you open Opening stock, so
   shoppers' phones never download them.
9. Plain screens, as you asked: existing admin cards, fields and buttons,
   no animations. One orange button per screen.

====================================================================
WHAT I SHOULD CHECK ON MY PHONE (step by step)
====================================================================
 1. Open Admin → More → Inventory. You should see "Inventory mode: Off",
    three tools (Opening stock, Needs weight, Compare stock) and "No lots
    yet".
 2. Tap Needs weight. You should see about 23 products with no size in
    the name (for example "Nivea Men Deep Espresso"). Type a weight for
    one (grams of one piece) and tap Save. It leaves the list.
 3. Tap "All products" there and check a few estimates (a 473 ml bottle
    should show 544 g). Correct any that are wrong.
 4. Go back. Tap New lot. Choose Import, currency AUD, type a rate
    (for example 80). Search and add 2 products. Type pieces and buy
    price (in AUD). Tap "Add weight cost" and type 5000.
    - If a product has no weight, the Preview shows "Enter weight for: ..."
      in red. Type its weight; the tick should come back.
    - Each product shows "Buy ৳... + weight ৳... + other ৳... = ৳... per
      piece".
    - At the bottom: "Allocated total = lot total ✓".
    Tap Create lot. You land on the lot page with the same numbers.
 5. On that lot page tap ⋮ → Edit lot, add an "other cost" (for example
    ৳500 customs), Save. The cost per piece goes up; the tick stays.
 6. Tap Compare stock. Your test lot's products now show "Lots N"; the
    difference to the shop's stock is shown. (Nothing changes the shop's
    stock.)
 7. Delete the test lot: Settings → Safety Locks → turn the lock off
    (password), then on the lot ⋮ → Delete lot → Delete lot.
 8. Open Inventory → Opening stock → Download template. Open it in Excel
    or Google Sheets on your phone or PC. Read the "How to fill" sheet.
 9. Fill 3–4 rows with real numbers, and on purpose make mistakes in two
    more rows: leave pieces empty on one, type -1 on another. Save as
    .xlsx and upload. You should see "To fix 2" with the rows listed, and
    Confirm greyed out.
10. Fix the file and upload again (it replaces the preview). Type your
    weight % (or open "Work it out from past lots"), pick the start date.
    Check total pieces and opening stock value. You may Confirm, or stop
    here and do the real upload later.
11. If you confirmed a test upload: Opening stock now shows "L-000 saved"
    and "Weight cost moved from old expenses into opening stock: ৳X".
    To remove it: turn off the Safety Lock, then "Undo opening stock".
12. Settings → Inventory mode: the switch is Off and greyed out, with
    "Available after Batch 39 (sales use lots)".
13. Place or look at a normal order: nothing about checkout or stock has
    changed.

INDUSTRY-LEVEL SUGGESTIONS (not built)
====================================================================
1. Stock count day (medium): a screen to count the shelf and record the
   difference as "adjustment" movements with a reason (damaged, expired,
   lost). Shops lose money quietly without a regular count.
2. Expiry alerts (small): a list of lots expiring in the next 3 months,
   so they can be sold on offer before they expire.
3. Exchange-rate memory (small): suggest the last rate used for each
   currency to save typing and mistakes.
4. Attach bill photos to a lot's costs (medium): proof for the accountant
   and for checking later.
5. Supplier list (small): pick suppliers from a list instead of typing,
   so reports by supplier are clean.
