BATCH 28 — Product page remaster: crystal glass buttons, rounded info
sheet, glass buy bar, one cart that follows you
====================================================================
Branch: feature/batch-28-pdp-remaster (made from the latest origin/main,
which already has Batch 27 merged). Pushed. NOT merged, nothing pushed
to main. No database changes and no Supabase deploys.


1. WHAT I DID
-------------

PART 1 — Top buttons: crystal glass
  - Only two buttons over the photo now: ← (left) and Share (right). The
    Cart button is gone from the product page header (the floating glass
    cart replaces it, Part 5).
  - Crystal glass: a thin dark tint (30%), blur(12px) saturate(150%), a
    bright hairline border (white 50%), an inner top highlight, a soft
    shadow, and a white line icon with a tiny drop shadow.
  - Visible on any photo. I measured it on swapped-in test photos:
      pure white photo  → icon vs. button contrast 2.0 : 1
      very light photo  → 2.2 : 1
      dark photo        → 18.5 : 1
    At the first try (20% tint) a pure white photo only gave 1.5 : 1 —
    too faint — so I used the "slightly darker inner tint" the batch
    allows (30%) and a stronger icon shadow. On white the buttons read as
    soft grey glass discs with white icons (see the white-photo
    screenshots).
  - Scrolled: the glass top bar with the product name still fades in.
    Its buttons keep the crystal style, tinted for a light (or dark) bar
    with a dark (or white) icon. Share now stays visible when scrolled
    (before it faded out) — with the cart gone, the bar would otherwise
    have had nothing on the right.
  - BUG FIX — orange glow on the right of the scrolled bar: the bar's
    glass was 82% white with a strong colour boost (saturate 1.6), so a
    colourful photo passing under it bled through as a coloured glow
    (and the orange cart badge sat right there too). The bar is now a
    near-solid neutral page tint (90%) with a plain blur, no colour boost,
    and the cart badge is gone from the header. Tested: an all-orange
    photo under the scrolled bar → zero orange pixels in the bar, light
    and dark.
  Proof: e2e/batch-28.spec.ts Part 1 (7 tests).

PART 2 — Rounded info sheet over the photo
  - The info area is a sheet: 28 px rounded top corners, overlaps the
    bottom of the photo by 24 px, very soft upward shadow, page colour
    (light: warm off-white #FAF8F5; dark: black). The whole product page
    background is that same colour, so the sheet reads as one surface.
  - No gradient anywhere on the photo (tested: nothing over the photo
    has a gradient, including ::before/::after).
  - Small centred grabber notch at the top of the sheet (decorative).
    The photo dots sit just under it, only with 2+ photos. The "1 / 3"
    pill on the photo was raised so the sheet never covers it.
  - Inside, in this order: brand (small, letter-spaced, grey, with ›,
    links to the brand page) → name (bold, 19.5 px, line-height 1.27,
    full name) → price row (big black/white price, struck-through old
    price, soft "Save x%" pill: red-orange text on a light tint) → chips
    ("● In stock" with a green dot and soft halo, + the product note) →
    region / size chips (only with 2+ options; the selected chip has a
    solid dark border; all Batch 23 rules unchanged) → the wholesale row
    (unchanged component, only for approved wholesalers/admins) → ONE
    trust plate with the 3 items and thin dividers (texts still from
    Admin → Banner & Texts → Site texts) → the sections (unchanged) →
    "You may also like" (unchanged).
  - There is no Add to Cart inside the page any more.
  Proof: e2e/batch-28.spec.ts Part 2 (4 tests).

  WAS THE OVERLAP SAFE WITH THE MORPH? Yes, so the overlap is kept.
  - The sheet is a sibling AFTER the photo, never inside it, so the
    morphing picture (same element, same view-transition-name, Batch 23
    corner logic untouched) never includes it. During the morph the
    flying picture is drawn above the whole page, so the sheet can't hide
    it either.
  - When the page is opened by the morph, the sheet starts flush with the
    bottom of the photo and slides up 24 px over it just after the photo
    lands (300 ms, starts at 340 ms; transform only). So the landing
    frame shows the whole photo and the overlap arrives as its own small,
    calm movement. Reduce-motion: no slide, the overlap is just there.
  - Back: the full photo shrinks back into its card as before.
  - Checked with frame captures (open and back), and all the existing
    morph / transition / phone-back e2e tests pass in all three runs.
  - No transition or scroll-memory code was edited (I only READ the
    "is a transition running?" flag).

PART 3 — Glass buy bar (the only place to buy)
  - Removed: the in-page Add to Cart, its "− 1 +" stepper and the Batch
    27 sticky bar.
  - One floating glass bar, shown from the moment the page opens: 12 px
    from the sides, 14 px above the bottom (+ safe area), 66 px tall,
    fully rounded. Strong glass: light white 60% / dark
    rgba(30,30,34,.45), blur(22px) saturate(160%), thin light border,
    inner top highlight, soft deep shadow. The page is visibly blurred
    behind it.
  - Layout 4 : 1 : 4 (≈ 44% : 11% : 44%): left = only the price, big and
    bold (no label, no thumbnail); middle = air; right = the action.
  - Action: not in the cart → orange "Add to Cart" pill with a cart icon.
    Tap → adds 1, haptic buzz, and the pill becomes, in exactly the same
    place and size (tested ±1 px), a "− 1 +" stepper (dark pill in light
    mode, light pill in dark mode, not orange); the floating cart jumps
    in or pulses.
  - The stepper always shows the real cart quantity of this product (or
    the selected option): + up to the stock count ("Only N in stock");
    − down; at 0 the item leaves the cart and Add to Cart comes back. It
    never flips back by itself (tested with a 4 s wait), and stays in
    sync with the cart page, the cards and after a reload (all tested).
  - Out of stock: a disabled grey "Out of stock" pill.
  - With options: the bar shows the selected option's price and that
    option's own cart quantity (tested with a pretend option).
  Proof: e2e/batch-28.spec.ts Part 3 (5 tests).

PART 4 — Buy bar scroll behaviour
  - Scroll down → the bar slides away (200 ms). Scroll up 10 px or more →
    it slides back. Always shown at the top of the page, at the very
    bottom, and right after any cart change (this product, an option, or
    a "You may also like" card). Small jitters never toggle it (the
    distance only adds up while you keep going one way). It never hides
    while your finger is on it. Transform + opacity only, same curve both
    ways; reduce-motion: plain show/hide with no slide.
  - Bottom padding grew so the bar never covers the last content ("You
    may also like" is fully visible above it — tested).
  Proof: e2e/batch-28.spec.ts Part 4 (2 tests).

PART 5 — One cart that follows you everywhere
  - The round floating glass cart is now the single cart button on the
    shop pages: Home, Search, All Brands, brand pages AND the product
    page. It appears with its jump-in bounce when the cart gets its first
    item and stays until the cart is empty. Never on the cart, checkout,
    account, orders or admin pages.
  - On the product page: same middle-right spot, a little smaller
    (52 px), never overlapping the buy bar or the top buttons (tested at
    390×844 and a small 360×640 phone). Every add AND remove there gives
    it a small pulse and updates its orange count; tapping opens the cart.
    (On the other pages it still pulses on adds only, as before.)
  - Crystal glass like Part 1 in light and dark (see decision below).
  - The bottom navigation's own Cart tab is unchanged.
  Proof: e2e/batch-28.spec.ts Part 5 (4 tests) + the updated Batch 27
  Part 2 tests + src/components/viewer/GlassCartButton.test.ts.

PART 6 — Small fixes
  1. Cart page: + now stops at the stock count with the same "Only N in
     stock" note (the option's own count for a line with an option; no
     limit when stock isn't tracked). The + looks faded at the limit.
  2. Cards, buy bar, floating cart badge and cart page all read the same
     cart, so they always show the same numbers (tested across all four).
  Proof: e2e/batch-28.spec.ts Part 6 + the sync test in Part 3.

4. WHAT I FOUND ALONG THE WAY
----------------------------
  - The floating cart sits at the middle of the right edge on purpose.
    On the product page, while the sheet is near that height, it can sit
    over the end of a long product name or a "You may also like" card
    until you scroll a little (same trade-off as on Home in Batch 27).
    Please check on your phone whether it bothers you; moving it lower
    (just above the buy bar) is an easy change.
  - FIXED a flaky test (an old one and a risk in my new ones): the
    site's offline helper (service worker) fetches the shop's data and
    photos by itself, and the test tool can't see or change requests it
    makes. Tests that add a pretend option or swap in a test photo
    therefore depended on how fast that helper took over the page. In my
    first set of three full runs, run 3 failed once in the Batch 27 test
    "an open option picker covers the glass cart" for exactly this reason
    (the pretend option never arrived). It is also why my first contrast
    numbers were measured on the real CeraVe photo instead of the test
    photos. The helper is now switched off for the Batch 27 and Batch 28
    test files (nothing in them tests offline mode), and the three full
    runs were redone from scratch on the final code.
  - The cart page's − at 1 still asks "Remove this item?" (unchanged);
    the buy bar's and the cards' − remove without asking. Both are
    reasonable, but they now behave differently.


5. DESIGN DECISIONS
-------------------
  - Header tint: 30% dark instead of 15–20% (contrast on white photos —
    numbers in Part 1). Still mostly see-through.
  - Floating cart glass: on Home it floats over white cards, where 10–14%
    white glass would be nearly invisible, so it uses white 30% (dark
    mode: dark 42%) with the same blur(12px) saturate(150%), hairline
    border, highlight and shadow as the header buttons, and a dark icon
    (white in dark mode).
  - Page colour: the product page (and its sheet) use a warm off-white
    #FAF8F5 as the batch describes; the rest of the site stays white.
  - "Save x%" pill colour: #D9381F on a 10% tint (dark mode #FF7461 on a
    14% tint) — clearly red-orange, but not the brand orange, so the only
    orange things on the page stay the Add to Cart pill and the count
    badge.
  - Share stays visible on the scrolled bar (see Part 1).
  - Wholesale row placed after the option chips, just before the trust
    plate (the batch's order didn't mention it; it belongs near the
    price information).
  - "You may also like" cards keep their own little cart buttons and
    steppers (unchanged, as asked) — they add those other products, not
    this one.
