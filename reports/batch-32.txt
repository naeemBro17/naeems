5. DESIGN DECISIONS
-------------------
  - Pay later is a separate column (collect_mode), not the old payment
    method "due", so existing "due" orders like NM-1721 stay exactly as
    they are until you change them (the brief's rule).
  - New order saves order + advance + mode in ONE database step
    (admin_create_order_v2 wraps the existing function), so an order never
    exists without its advance. The old payment column is still filled
    (bKash / Cash when fully paid, Due for pay later, else COD) so the
    invoice and older screens keep reading sensibly.
  - "How was it paid?" has no default: you must tap one when an amount is
    typed — a wrong default would record the wrong method silently.
  - Edit order's "Paid now" becomes "Add a payment now" when earlier
    payments exist, and shows "Paid before"; it needs "Change order
    status", the same switch as adding a payment on the order.
  - "Discard changes?" counts anything typed or chosen in a field; a
    customer's note saved with its own Save button counts as saved.
  - Customers are keyed by the same key as Batch 30's grouping, so nothing
    about existing customers or orders changes; Hide also follows the phone
    (a hand-added customer who later makes an account stays hidden).
  - Home's due total counts hidden customers too (money owed is still
    owed); the Customers "Due" filter therefore also shows hidden ones, so
    the two numbers always match.
  - Splash icon at 62 % size: inside Android's round crop, still large.
