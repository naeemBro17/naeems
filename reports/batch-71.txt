6. INDUSTRY-LEVEL SUGGESTIONS
------------------------------------------------------------

1. Real skin type and concern filters (medium). The tiles search by word
   today; a product that does not say "Oily" in its name will not appear.
   Shoppers who tap a concern and see few results leave. Batch 72 plans it.
2. Product price colour (small). Make card prices near-black as the design
   rule says; orange prices on every card compete with the one orange
   button and read cheaper.
3. Best sellers from real sales (medium). "Featured" is hand-picked; once
   orders are stored, ranking by units sold in the last 30 days is more
   honest and keeps the row fresh.
4. Reviews or star ratings on cards (large). The single strongest trust
   signal before buying; there is none on Home or Shop today.
5. Bento clean-up (small). Remove or re-place the bento admin so you do not
   edit something nobody sees.
