THE ADVANCE-PAYMENT DISCOUNT (৳1,000 RULE)
 • The bKash / Nagad discount is given only when ৳1,000 or more is paid
   in advance for that order. Below that, no "Pay in advance" badge and
   no discount (bKash / Nagad still work). Wallet money never earns it:
   the wallet part comes off first, then the rule looks at what is left.
   The server decides; the screen only shows a preview. Old orders keep
   exactly the discount they were placed with (nothing recalculated).
 • Settings preview now says "The customer saves ৳50 when ৳1,000 or more
   is paid by bKash or Nagad."

WHAT YOU DO WHEN "I SENT MONEY" COMES ON WHATSAPP
 • Home → Needs attention shows "৳3,000 wallet money from Ayesha to
   confirm". Tap it (or open the customer) → WALLET card (above TO
   RECEIVE) → check the money really arrived → Confirm. A small sheet asks
   which account it came into (bKash for bKash; Nagad for Nagad, or bKash
   if you have no Nagad account) → Confirm. The money goes into that
   account AND into the customer's wallet. Reject (reason optional) moves
   no money.

ADDING MONEY FOR A CUSTOMER YOURSELF
 • Customer page → WALLET → "Add to wallet": Amount, Received in (your
   accounts, never "With Steadfast"), Date (today), Note → "Add ৳5,000".
   Works for customers with no login too.
 • Super Admin only: "Correct" (new balance, a reason, your password) for
   mistakes. No money moves; written to the Activity Log.
 • New order and Edit order: if the customer has a balance, a "Use wallet
   balance" switch. It takes up to what the order still owes.

HOW IT SHOWS ON THE MONEY PAGE
 • A wallet balance is money the business OWES the customer. It is never a
   sale and never profit (profit still counts only when an order is
   delivered; Batch 58's profit engine is untouched).
 • Money added: the account goes up AND "Customer wallets" (inside To
   give) goes up by the same amount, so the balance check stays green.
 • Wallet used on an order: no account moves; the order's due goes down
   and "Customer wallets" goes down by the same amount.
 • "Investors and what I owe → Other people" lists "Customer wallets", and
   "Show the sums" says "of it Customer wallets: ৳X" (only when not ৳0).
   Home's "To give" includes it.
 • Steadfast COD = what is still owed after the wallet. An order fully
   paid from the wallet is booked with COD ৳0 (also after an edit or a
   re-book: COD always reads the order's current due).

THE AUTOMATIC PART
 • Order cancelled / returned / edited down below what was paid: the
   wallet part goes back to the wallet; anything paid above the total by
   real money also goes into the wallet ("Extra paid on order"). Batch
   58's "Credit — paid more than owed" line is gone: that money is the
   wallet now. Today there are 0 such orders, so the migration moves ৳0.
 • Order edited up again / reopened: taken back from the wallet as far as
   the balance allows (the rest is due).
 • Order deleted (Super Admin + Safety Lock): its wallet money goes back.
 • Merging two customers (Batch 55): the wallet moves with them; the total
   does not change.

5. DESIGN DECISIONS
======================================================================
 • Who may Confirm / Reject / Add: "View profit & costs", the same
   permission that records a customer's payments on the customer page.
   Correct: Super Admin + password. Using the wallet on an admin order:
   "Create orders" or "Edit orders".
 • ৳1,000 is compared with what is left BEFORE the discount (so a ৳1,000
   advance gets it; the test "৳1,000 gets it" needs this).
 • With wallet + bKash, the percent discount is worked out on the
   products' part of what is left (never delivery, never the wallet).
 • Fully paid from the wallet: saved as Cash on Delivery with nothing to
   pay (no bKash payment to check). Screens say "Wallet".
 • A transaction ID is "used" forever once in any request (also a
   rejected one) or any order payment, as the brief says.
 • Phone-number wallets follow the number: if a no-login customer later
   signs up with that number, their wallet comes with them.
 • A customer with a wallet can't be deleted (hide instead).
 • Toggle on checkout: the site's existing green switch style.
 • Tab title "My Wallet | NAEEM'S" (no long dash in customer text).
