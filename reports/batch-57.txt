PART 1 — MAP OF EVERY MONEY NUMBER
======================================================================
Common building blocks (live versions):
 • Sale date = when the order became Delivered (last history row to
   "delivered"; if there is none, orders.updated_at). Returned orders
   count on the day they were returned. A cancelled order that Steadfast
   charged counts that charge on the payout day.
 • Sale = items − discount (promo + advance discount are both inside
   orders.discount). Delivery charged = orders.delivery_fee. Both only when
   Delivered.
 • Cost = order_item_costs.unit_cost_bdt × quantity (the stored cost per
   line, from lots, or "Set buying prices" when no lot).
 • Courier = order_courier_costs.total_kept (the real Steadfast bill). If
   there is none: delivered + booked → EXPECTED fee (average of the last
   10 inside/outside-Dhaka bills, or the Settings fee if there are fewer
   than 3, + 1% COD fee); hand delivery → ৳0; never booked → ৳0.
 • "Costed" order = every line has a stored cost. An order NOT costed is
   left out of all totals (its sale, delivery and courier).
 • Money only: an order placed before 13 Nov and not taken from lots is
   treated as not costed (its sale was in the opening balance).

Screen → function → what it includes / date / leaves out
 Home "Your profit" (period today/month/year): money_dashboard →
   money_summary → money_profit_parts. Sales profit (as above) − expenses −
   write-offs − investor shares. Leaves out: old Kaarbar sales; expenses /
   shares before 13 Nov (sales before 13 Nov are IN). "You took" =
   withdrawals; "Left in the business" = Your profit − withdrawals (a
   profit number, not cash).
 Home "Return on money": net profit ÷ average net worth (opening + profit
   since start). Shows "—" today because net worth is negative (M1).
 Home "Money now": money_position. Accounts = money_ledger (movements +
   order payments + previous-balance payments + Steadfast fees / payouts,
   all from 13 Nov). With Steadfast = COD collected − fees − payouts placed.
   To receive = order dues (Cancelled/Returned owe 0) + previous balances.
   To give = investor capital (opening + in − back) + supplier lots unpaid
   + other people. Stock value = units left × landed cost. The balance badge
   = the books balance check (C2).
 Money page: accounts / movements / expenses (money_ledger,
   money_movements from 13 Nov), investors and suppliers
   (money_to_give_rows; share this month/year have NO start-date filter,
   share all time has one — I8), customer dues (money_customer_dues: order
   dues per customer + previous balance left; an overpaid order counts as
   ৳0 — I11), month lock (triggers on 13 tables — I10).
 Reports → Profit & loss: report_profit_loss = money_profit_parts +
   kaarbar_old_parts (old Kaarbar bills by bill date: items − bill discount
   − cost from Kaarbar items + delivery; a line with no cost would count
   ৳0 cost — there are none today). Expenses by category: only from 13 Nov.
 Reports → Sales: report_sales. Orders PLACED in the dates (not
   cancelled), totals include delivery; returned count 0. The screen says
   so. It is not profit.
 Reports → Products / Customers: inventory_sales_profit (the Inventory
   rule: no start-date treatment, so the 4 orders of I2 are IN) + old
   Kaarbar lines (Products).
 Reports → Lots: report_lots (all time; units sold / on orders / left,
   sales and cost of delivered units).
 Reports → Charts: money_profit_parts per month (no old Kaarbar sales —
   differs from P&L months that have old bills).
 Reports → Investors / investor PDF: report_investors (capital and shares
   from 13 Nov) and report_investor_statement (monthly sales / net profit
   from money_profit_parts; footer says "Books kept since 13 Nov", but the
   October row shows profit with no expenses — C1).
 Inventory → Sales profit: inventory_sales_profit (no expenses; includes
   orders placed before 13 Nov — I2; shows the expected/real courier label).
 Inventory → lots / landed cost: landed per unit = (buy value + its share
   of the lot's OTHER costs, shared by value) ÷ units. Weight is not in it
   (Batch 45). Lot owed = landed + lot payments (lots dated before 13 Nov
   with no payment owe ৳0).
 Inventory → Set buying prices: product_buying_prices; fills only lines
   that have NO cost yet.
 Inventory → shortfalls: stock_shortfalls (provisional cost per unit,
   then real cost when a lot fills it). The cost stored on the sale is
   refreshed to the lot's cost; "goods on open orders" is not (C2).
 Write-offs: stock_write_offs.cost_bdt, counted on write_date (no
   start-date filter — I9).
 Order Profit card: order_profit. Same rules as Sales profit, except a
   CANCELLED order always shows courier ৳0 (I7).
 Customer page dues: orders' dues + previous balance (opening dues +
   corrections − lump-sum payments). Old Kaarbar bills are shown for
   information only (their unpaid part is the previous balance — checked:
   the merged customer's ৳5,950 is counted once, not twice).
 Steadfast payouts: payout lines → order_courier_costs per order (exact =
   1 parcel, share = split). Fees reduce "With Steadfast"; placing a payout
   moves the net into an account. "Bill paid separately" counts in profit
   but never moves money (I6).

What the balance check compares, and is it right?
 It compares net worth today with the opening + profit since the start,
 plus owner money and corrections. That is the right accounting identity
 (change in net worth = profit − withdrawals + owner money in). Two
 weaknesses:
 (1) it adds an "outside" term for orders not counted in profit. That makes
     the check balance even though those sales are missing from profit, so
     it can't catch I2 — by design;
 (2) amounts in profit with no matching "owed" line (expected courier,
     separately paid bills, provisional costs) turn it red when nothing is
     wrong in your entries (C2).
 Investor capital in/back moves cash and "to give" equally, so it doesn't
 change net worth. That is correct the way the site counts capital (as a
 debt).

   harmless, but it means a "read" isn't really read-only. My scripts wrap
   it in a transaction that is undone.
 • A shipped order can't be cancelled on the site (only pending /
   confirmed). A parcel Steadfast cancels after pickup must be marked
   Returned. That is fine, but worth knowing for courier charges.
 • lot L-000's cost was lowered in Batch 45 (weight taken out). Open
   orders taken before that still carry the old cost (NM-3788 ৳688.75 vs
   ৳475) — part of C2.
 • The admin order form's custom price key is "sold_price" (an item with
   "unit_price" is silently priced at the list price). That is not a bug in
   the app, only in how a script could call it.
 • package-lock.json still says 1.50.1. Earlier batches left it too; I
   didn't touch it.

5. DESIGN DECISIONS
======================================================================
 • I read the LIVE function bodies instead of trusting the migration files
   (CLAUDE.md: never assume a migration ran). Batches 55.1 and 56 are on
   the live database.
 • The made-up month measures CHANGES (after − before) so it never needs
   to touch or hide a real row. The made-up opening balance is written
   directly (the real books are already confirmed). Everything else goes
   through the site's own functions.
 • The checkout orders use the e2e test customer login (no real
   customer). Order alerts can't fire: the Telegram trigger only queues a
   request, and the queue sends only after a COMMIT.
 • Severity: "Critical" = a number on screen is wrong today. "Important" =
   two screens disagree, or it goes wrong in a normal situation.
   "Minor" = wording / clarity.
 • Tests: no app code changed, so no browser tests and no full
   `npm run verify` (the brief says so). `npm run build`: passed, zero
   errors. The two SQL scripts were run on the live database inside undone
   transactions; each takes a few seconds.

6. INDUSTRY-LEVEL SUGGESTIONS (not done; highest impact first)
======================================================================
 1. One profit engine (M). Today 3 different sums answer "profit". A
    professional system has ONE function for profit by period. Every
    screen (Home, P&L, Charts, investor PDF, Products, Customers) reads it,
    with a clear "books start" date. This removes C1, I1 and I2 at once.
 2. A proper "owed" side for accrued costs (S–M). Expected courier fees,
    bills paid separately and agreed-but-unpaid investor shares should
    each be an amount owed, so the balance light only goes red for real
    mistakes. An accountant calls these accruals.
 3. Freeze each month's numbers when you lock it (M). Store the month's
    P&L as a snapshot at lock time and compare later recalculations with
    it. A locked month must never change silently (I10).
 4. Never delete a delivered order (S). Allow void / return only, so cash
    and sales history can't disappear.
 5. Separate investors' capital from debts (S). Show "Investors' money in
    the business" apart from "To give", so net worth means what you own.
 6. Customer credit (S). Show overpayments as credit on the customer and
    let it pay the next order.
 7. Courier payable for "not booked" deliveries (S). If you use another
    rider or courier, record its cost on the order so profit isn't
    overstated.
 8. A month-end checklist report (M). One page per month: profit, cash
    moves, balance check, unplaced payouts, expected fees older than 14
    days, open shortfalls. Lock only when it is clean. This is what an
    accountant does every month.
