FIX 71.1 - theme button, liquid bottom bar, smooth tab change, Search keyboard, cart bubble taps
Branch: fix/batch-71-1-nav-polish   Version: 1.71.1

1. WHAT I DID

Theme button (Home, top right): the sun/moon icon is now 19 px with a thin
line instead of filling the whole circle. When tapped, the new icon turns in
softly (only on a tap, never when the page opens).

Bottom bar: every tab now has a fixed width (52 px, or 120 px for the active
one), so the bar never changes width or shifts sideways. One glass pill sits
behind the tabs and slides to the new tab like liquid: it stretches on the
way, squashes a little and settles with a small spring. The other icons glide
to their new places, the label fades in after the pill, and a tap gives a
small press. The light bar is now slightly grey so the white pill shows on it.

Tab change: Home / Shop / Search taps now use their own softer crossfade
(old page out in 0.2 s, new page in over 0.28 s). The bar keeps its own place
during the change, so the pill's slide is seen instead of a blink.

Search keyboard: the bottom bar's Search tab opens the Search page with the
keyboard down. The search pills on Home and Shop (and the old search entry
bar) still open it with the keyboard up, because the shopper tapped a box.

Cart bubble: it now sits above the bottom bar (it used to be under it, so
taps where they overlap went to the bar). A tap with a slightly moving thumb
(up to 10 px) counts as a tap, not a drag. After a drag, the next tap is no
longer swallowed. When moved low, it stops above the real bar and moves back
above it when the bar returns.

4. WHAT I FOUND ALONG THE WAY

- On Chromium (desktop test browser, dark mode), while any page crossfade
  runs, a faint hard-edged rectangle shows around the rounded bar for about
  0.3 s. It is the bar's frosted (blurred) background, drawn without its
  rounded corners. It shows both with and without the bar's own transition
  name, so it comes from the page crossfade itself, not from this fix. I did
  not check whether Batch 71's old crossfade had it too. Please watch for it
  on the phone. If it bothers you on the real device, a small follow-up can
  turn the bar's blur off only during the change.

5. DESIGN DECISIONS

- Kept the bar's own view-transition name (the main plan in the brief). I
  tried the fallback (dropping the four b71-nav lines): it did not remove the
  rectangle above, and it lost the "bar stays still" benefit, so I put the
  main plan back.
- Frame checks (pictures in reports/fix-71-1-screens/):
  - theme-button-dark-before.png: a small thin moon, well inside the circle.
    theme-button-after-tap.png: the new icon after its turn.
  - The pill's place was recorded on every frame for 400 ms after a
    Home -> Shop tap, in dark and in light. It was seen in at least 3 places
    between the two tabs, and the bar's blur was set on every frame.
    pill-dark-step1..4.png and pill-light-step1..4.png show the slide back to
    Home with animations slowed 10x for the pictures. Step 1 shows the pill

    the bar. The test also checks that it goes lower when the bar hides and
    moves back above it when the bar returns.

6. INDUSTRY-LEVEL SUGGESTIONS

- (Small) The cart bubble and the bar now share the bottom of the screen. A
  professional app usually puts the cart in the bar itself (a badge on a Cart
  tab), so nothing floats over the tabs. That is worth a look once the new
  bar has settled.
