2. WHAT STEADFAST'S PAYOUT DATA CONTAINS
============================================================
  Read live (after your yes), so these are the REAL fields:
    One payout (GET /payments, a list of 10 per page, OLDEST first, with
    no page count — the sync keeps asking for the next page until a page
    brings nothing new):
      payment_id   "SFC-29776606"
      paid_at      the date and time it was paid (Dhaka time)
      amount       COD collected in that payout          e.g. ৳2,390
      charges      the COD fees for the whole payout      e.g. ৳22
      due_bills    the delivery charges for the payout   e.g. ৳230
      total        what reached you                      e.g. ৳2,138
                   (2,390 − 22 − 230 = 2,138, exactly)
      method       "Bkash" · status_label "paid" · paid_bills (a third
                   amount, ৳425 in the example — its meaning isn't
                   documented; kept in the raw answer, not used)
    One parcel inside a payout (GET /payments/{id}):
      consignment_id, invoice, cod_amount, recipient name / phone /
      address, status, tracking_code, dates. NO delivery charge and NO
      COD fee per parcel.
  So: the payout totals are exact (all 26 payouts add up to the paisa).
  Each ORDER'S share of the fees is worked out from the payout totals:
  the COD fee is split by each order's COD, and the delivery charges by
  each order's own delivery fee (inside/outside Dhaka) where it is our
  order, otherwise equally. They are marked as estimates. Per order it is
  close, not to the paisa; per payout and per month it is exact.
  How far back: 26 payouts, 14 Jan 2026 – 3 Oct 2026 (everything
  Steadfast lists).
  Not matched: all 142 parcels in those payouts were booked by hand on
  Steadfast's website. Your first booking from the site was on 5 Oct,
  after the newest payout (3 Oct), so none of your site orders has been
  paid yet. The ৳3,445 balance is waiting for them. Parcels whose invoice
  is not one of our order numbers (empty, "N/A" or Steadfast's own code)
  are labelled "Booked directly on Steadfast, not from this site" in
  grey. They never count as "to check" and their payout gets a grey
  "Booked on Steadfast" pill. Only parcels with one of our NM- numbers
  can be Matched or To check.

3. THE MONEY RULE IN PLAIN WORDS
============================================================
  The customer pays the rider the COD → Steadfast keeps its delivery
  charge and a 1% COD fee → Steadfast pays you the rest in a payout.
  Example: order NM-1722, COD ৳2,299.00
     delivery charge  − ৳130.00
     COD fee (1%)     −  ৳22.99
     you receive        ৳2,146.01   (shown as ৳2,146)
  Nothing is counted twice: the "COD via Steadfast" payment row (Batch
  30) is still the record that the customer paid. A payout is only
  Steadfast handing that money to you. It is never a second payment.
  Each order's courier cost (charge + fee + other) and the amount you
  received are kept in order_courier_costs, ready for the later
  profit/inventory batch.

5. THE PARSER (Smart paste)
============================================================
  Handles:
  - Phones: 01XXXXXXXXX with or without +88 / 88, spaces, dashes, dots,
    Bengali digits (০–৯). A second number → Alternative phone.
  - Labels (English or Bengali): Name, Phone/Mobile, Address, Thana/PS,
    District, COD/Price, Product/Item. COD and Product are shown as
    hints only ("In the message (not added): COD 1250 tk · Product:
    Scalpe Plus x2"). Nothing is added to the order by itself.
  - Name: the "Name:" label, otherwise the first line with no digits
    that doesn't look like an address or a place. Kept as written.
  - Thana: matched against our thana list (Steadfast's list when it has
    loaded, otherwise the bundled one), with Banglish spelling tolerance
    (o/a, sh/s, oo/u, ph/f, kh/k, double letters, "gonj"/"ganj",
    "sadar"/"model" ignored) and Bengali script names. The district in
    the message decides between same-named thanas.
  - It only picks a thana when the match is clear. Otherwise it picks
    nothing and offers the top 3 as chips.
  - Address: the customer's own words. Only spaces and commas are
    tidied, and the phone/name lines are removed. It is never translated
    or reworded.
  Example results:
    "Rume / …pisone basha,uttar bazar,poroshuram, Feni / 01638820872"
        → Rume · 01638820872 · Parshuram, Feni (district Feni)
    "Mirpur 10, Dhaka"        → Mirpur (Dhaka), not Kushtia's Mirpur
    "Mirpur" alone            → nothing picked; chips incl. Dhaka and Kushtia
    "Sector 7, Uttara, Dhaka" → nothing picked; chips Uttara East / West
    "station road, Feni"      → no thana; chips Feni Sadar, Chhagalnaiya, …
    "sitakundo, chittagong"   → Sitakunda, Chattogram
    Bengali "উত্তর বাজার, পরশুরাম, ফেনী" → Parshuram, Feni
  Can't do: guess a thana that isn't written at all (only the district),
  tell apart two areas with the same name when no district is given,
  understand free sentences ("send to my sister's house"), or read
  products/quantities into the order.

6. DECISIONS, WHAT ONLY YOUR PHONE CAN CONFIRM, LATER BATCHES
============================================================
  Decisions I made:
  - Address: kept the thana and district words inside the address
    (the mockup's filled address had dropped them and capitalised "Uttar
    Bazar"). The brief says "never reworded, only remove the phone/name
    parts", and riders read the address, so the customer's full words
    stay.
  - 7-day alert: counts only Steadfast orders delivered after the oldest
    payout we hold. Orders delivered before that were paid before the
    history Steadfast still shows, and would otherwise warn forever. It
    shows nothing until the first sync has run.
  - Courier charges in "This month" include any "other" amount Steadfast
    kept (return charge etc.), so the four lines always add up.
  - Smart paste's "reading" lasts 2.4 s. The text shimmer is done with
    fading only, because the site animates only movement and fading
    (the demo's gradient sweep would break that rule).
  - The orb and the flower are staggered (orb out by 0.2 s, flower in
    from 0.22 s, and the mirror of that going back), so they can never
    overlap, not even for one frame.
  - The editor keeps an empty line after a box so you can keep typing
    under it; that empty line is not saved.
  - The Refresh button is grey, not orange, as in the mockup: the payouts
    page is for reading, with no main action. On New order, Smart
    paste's orange is "Fill the form", as in the mockup.
  Only your phone/PC can confirm:
  - How the orb/flower feels on your phone, and the typing speed.
  - Pasting from real WhatsApp/Word on your phone (tests use simulated
    pastes).
  - The real Steadfast payout field names and history (section 8).
  - The photo corners on your real photos with your screen brightness.
  Needs a later batch:
  - Profit per order (price − cost − courier costs). The courier half is
    now stored.
  - A "Mark as checked" button for "to check" payout rows.
  - Smart paste for products ("Scalpe Plus x2" → add the item).
