Photo counter. Every product with more than one photo now shows the
same dark glass pill with "current / total" (1 / 2, 1 / 5, 1 / 11 ...),

dots and the plain white "3 / 20" text from Fix 72.1 are gone, together

swipe away from the photo. Screen readers hear "Photo 2 of 5" when they
reach it, but it does not announce itself on every swipe.

sits on the photo, not on the page.

unchanged), same place, same outline when not saved and filled when
saved, same tap behaviour.

The heart drawing is shared with two other places (the "Saved to your
wishlist" message at the bottom of the screen and the Wishlist page).
Those keep the old shape: I added a "round" switch to the heart drawing
and only the product photo's heart turns it on.

Tests. The old tests that looked for dots or the plain text now check
the new rule (pill when more than one photo, nothing for one photo).
No test was deleted. A new test file (e2e/fix-72-2.spec.ts) checks

4. WHAT I FOUND ALONG THE WAY
==========================================================================

- Position: the fix text said "bottom: 12px". The rounded white sheet
  under the photo overlaps the photo's bottom by 24 px, so a plain
  12px would have put the pill half under the sheet. I used
  "24 px + 12 px", which puts it exactly 12 px above the photo's
  visible bottom edge, as you asked (the test measures it: 12 px).
- On a pure white photo the pill turns mid-grey and the white numbers
  are readable but low in contrast (roughly 2 to 1). On normal product
  photos and dark photos it reads well. If you want it stronger on
  white photos, raising the glass darkness from 0.30 to about 0.45 fixes
  it; I kept your exact values.
- Same white-photo problem, older and not touched here: the Back, Share
  and heart glass circles almost disappear on a pure white photo
  (screens: after-bright-photo-360-light.png).
- Dead CSS removed: besides the Fix 72.1 dot and text rules, app.css
  still had ".product-detail__dots / __dot" rules from an early batch
  that nothing used any more. Deleted. The small dots on product CARDS
  (a different thing, "--card-dots-...") are untouched.
- Nothing else used the photo dots.
- A test bug, not a page bug: batch-27's "no theme button in the
  header" test looked for any header button whose label has "light" or
  "dark" in it. The Share button's label includes the product name, and
  the test product is "Aveeno ... Lightly Scented ...", so it failed. I
  changed the test to look for the toggle's real labels ("... dark
  mode" / "... light mode" / "theme"). It passes. The page is unchanged.

==========================================================================
6. INDUSTRY-LEVEL SUGGESTIONS
==========================================================================

1. Glass buttons on very light photos (medium impact, small): many
   skincare photos have white backgrounds. A faint dark edge or slightly
   darker glass on Back / Share / heart when the photo is light would
   keep them findable. Customers who cannot find Back feel lost.
2. Tap the pill to see all photos as a grid (low impact, medium): big
   shops let you jump straight to photo 8 of 11 instead of swiping
   seven times.
