1. WHAT I DID
-------------

PART 1 — Product page redesign
  Top of the page:
  - The header now floats over the photo: ← on the left; Share and a
    Cart button with an item-count badge on the right, all round glass
    buttons. Tapping Cart opens the cart. The light/dark switch is gone
    from this header only (it is still on Home, brand pages, etc.).
  - Gallery: same swipe gallery, dots under the photo and a "1 / 3" pill
    (only when there is more than one photo). The photo is still the
    morph target: same element, same view-transition-name, still no
    rounded corners of its own. I did not touch any transition or
    scroll-memory code.
  - Brand name (small, letter-spaced, underlined, with a ›) above the
    title, still a link to the brand page.
  - Title large and bold, always in full.
  - Price large and black, struck-through old price, red "Save x%".
  - Size/region chips: the selected one now has a black border on a
    white chip. All Batch 23 option rules unchanged (photo follows the
    choice, ?variant= links, etc.).
  - Stock line: green dot + "In stock · <the product's note>".
  - Three trust boxes with line icons: "100% authentic", "Cash on
    delivery", "Fast delivery". Editable in Admin → Banner & Texts →
    Site texts (new group "Product page trust boxes"). They are stored as
    normal settings rows, so NO database change was needed. Empty =
    default text.
  - Quantity stepper (− 1 +) next to Add to Cart. Minimum 1. Maximum =
    stock left (stock count minus what is already in the cart) for
    products that track stock; up to 99 when stock isn't tracked. Tapping
    + at the limit shows "Only N in stock". If the whole stock is already
    in the cart the button says "All in your cart" and is disabled.
  - Add to Cart adds the chosen quantity, shows "Added ✓" for a moment,
    buzzes (where phones support it), and the header cart badge pulses.
  - Wholesale row: untouched component, same rules (only approved
    wholesalers and admins).
  Scrolled part:
  - Once the photo has scrolled away, a glass top bar fades in behind
    the buttons with the product name on one line (…); Share fades out.
  - Once the main Add to Cart has scrolled up out of view, a glass
    sticky bar slides up from the bottom: price (+ size underneath when
    the product has one) and an orange Add to Cart (adds the same
    quantity). It hides again when the main button is back on screen.
    The page has bottom padding so the bar never covers the last content.
  - Sections: About this product · How to use · Key ingredients · Video
    review. Clean rows, thin dividers, + / −. Several can be open. "About
    this product" starts open (or "How to use" when there is no About).
  - FIX — blue tap box: removed everywhere on the shop
    (-webkit-tap-highlight-color: transparent on the whole page). Rows get
    a soft grey pressed state instead.
  - FIX — page jump when opening/closing a section: the tapped row's
    header is now held at exactly the same place on screen while the
    content changes (the browser's "scroll anchoring" was moving it). The
    content fades and slides in under it (opacity + transform only);
    closing is the mirror. A closed section's content is removed, so a
    playing video stops when you close its section.
  - Video review: before play, while starting, while paused and after it
    ends, only OUR cover shows: YouTube's thumbnail, our round white Play
    button and a small "Reviewed on YouTube" label. The cover goes up the
    instant Pause is tapped (before YouTube answers) and also in the last
    0.6 s before the end, so YouTube's pause screen and "more videos"
    end screen never get a frame. All Batch 23 no-exit rules kept
    (inline, no fullscreen, a full-size invisible layer takes every tap).
  - "You may also like" at the end (Part 4).
  Proof: e2e/batch-27.spec.ts Part 1 tests (list under TESTS), and the
  screenshots.

PART 2 — Glass cart button (Home, Search, All Brands, brand pages)
  - Round 60 px glass button at the middle of the right edge with an
    orange count badge, shown while the cart has something in it.
  - Jumps in with a short bounce when the cart goes from empty to 1 on
    that page; every later add gives a small pulse and the count updates.
    Reduce-motion: no bounce or pulse, it just appears.
  - Tapping it opens the cart. Gone when the cart is empty.
  - Not shown on the product page, cart, checkout, account, orders or
    admin. It sits above the page but below the bottom nav, sheets and
    dialogs (an open option picker covers it — tested).
  - Glass: semi-transparent white (dark: semi-transparent dark grey),
    blur, a soft light border and a soft shadow, in both modes.

PART 3 — Card cart button → quantity stepper; no brand line on cards
  - The cards had no brand line any more (nothing to remove); the test
    checks it stays that way.
  - Cart button is now neutral: light grey circle with a dark icon (dark
    mode: dark grey circle with a light icon).
  - Tap → added (1) and the button becomes a "− 1 +" pill (dark pill in
    light mode, light pill in dark mode). + adds one up to the stock
    limit ("Only N in stock" message at the limit). − removes one; at 0
    the item leaves the cart and the cart button comes back.
  - The number is read from the cart itself, so it always matches the
    cart page, the product page and other cards of the same product.
  - Products with options still open the "Choose options" sheet; after
    adding, the card's stepper shows that option's quantity.
  - The old green tick is gone; the haptic buzz stays.
  - Card price is now orange (the batch text: "orange only on the price"
    on cards), since the cart button lost its orange.

PART 4 — "You may also like"
  The exact rule as built (src/lib/relatedProducts.ts):
   1. Start from the catalogue the site already loaded. Remove the
      current product, hidden products and out-of-stock products.
   2. Group 1: same category AND same brand. Group 2: same category.
      Group 3: same brand. (Brand = brand id; for an old product without
      a brand id, the brand name, ignoring capitals and spaces.)
   3. Inside each group, the closest price first (the price shown on the
      card); a tie goes alphabetically.
   4. Take up to 6.
   5. If fewer than 3 matched, fill up (to 6) with the newest other
      in-stock products. (The batch text said "same category group or
      the newest"; the shop has no category groups, so it is the newest.)
   6. If fewer than 2 in the end, the section is hidden.
  - One sideways row using the browser's own scrolling (no touch code, so
    up/down scrolling with a finger on the row works normally).
  - Small cards with the Part 3 rules. On the product page their price is
    black (orange on this page is only Add to Cart).
  - Tapping a card opens that product with a plain quick fade (no picture
    morph from this row, so it can never interfere with the main grid's
    morph). Back (phone or ←) returns to the previous product at the same
    scroll position — tested.

PART 5 — Card titles rebuilt from scratch
  The real reason the title never used the card's full width: the cart
  button had its own column BESIDE the text, so the title only got the
  card width minus about 42 px. CSS word-break tweaks could never fix
  that.
  Deleted:
  - the old title rules: white-space / word-break: normal /
    overflow-wrap: normal / hyphens: none / min-height on
    .product-card__name;
  - the two-column card body (.product-card__info text column + the
    34 px .product-card__cart-slot column beside it);
  - the leftover comment pointing to the old JS splitter (textWrap.ts was
    already gone since Batch 7 — no JS measuring or splitting names
    exists anywhere now).
  New: the title is a plain block, 100% of the card's text area,
  -webkit-line-clamp: 2 + overflow-wrap: break-word, normal word
  wrapping, line-height 1.35, a fixed 2-line height. The card is now:
  title (full width) → price → bottom row with "In stock" on the left and
  the cart button / stepper on the right in its own grid slot (never over
  the text). Same component on Home, Search, brand pages and "You may
  also like".
  Proof: the e2e test measures, at 360, 390 and 412 px, light and dark,
  on the first 12 search results for "aveeno": title width = card content
  width (±1 px), every first line is full (the next word really would
  not have fitted), and every title box is the same height.
  Screenshots: reports/batch-27-screens/titles-<width>-<light|dark>.png.
  Note: a very long name (e.g. "Aveeno Baby Daily Moisture Lightly
  Scented Wash & Shampoo 236mL") needs more than 2 lines at phone width,
  so it still ends with "…" after the second line — that is the
  "truncate only if longer than two lines" rule. The full name is on the
  product page.

4. WHAT I FOUND ALONG THE WAY
----------------------------
  - The floating glass cart sits at the middle of the right edge, as
    designed. While a right-hand card is exactly mid-screen, the glass
    button covers that card's cart button / stepper until you scroll a
    little. That is the trade-off of this position — please check on your
    phone whether it bothers you.
  - The live catalogue has no products with options today (they were
    split into separate products in Batch 24). The option tests therefore
    add one pretend option to the page's own copy of the data (the
    database is never touched), so they always run.
  - The cart page itself has no stock limit on its + button (it never
    had). The card and product page now respect stock; the cart page
    could too in a later batch.
  - The old "Video Review" section used to keep playing sound after its
    section was closed (the content was only hidden). Fixed as a side
    effect: a closed section's content is removed.
  - The big loading spinner is orange everywhere on the site; on the
    video cover it is white now.
  - FIXED a small admin bug (Admin → Banner & Texts → Site texts): if the
    shop's data reloaded just after the page opened (it does when your
    sign-in is confirmed), whatever you had already typed in a text box was
    wiped, and Save then saved an empty box (= back to the default text).
    Now a box you have typed in is never overwritten until you save. This
    is what made the Batch 24 "checkout sign-in note" test fail now and
    then; it now passes 5 out of 5 on repeat.
  - Video play/pause now follows the tap at once (the label and our cover
    change on the tap itself), then settles to what YouTube reports.
    Before, after very fast taps the button could briefly show the wrong
    word. Found while making the old Batch 23 video test pass reliably;
    it is now 12 out of 12 on repeat.


5. DESIGN DECISIONS
-------------------
  - Mockup vs text: the text won where they differ:
    · Card prices: the mockup and the text both show orange card prices,
      but CLAUDE.md says prices are black. I followed this batch's text
      (orange price, neutral cart button = still one orange thing per
      card). On the product page all prices are black.
    · "the glass cart appears" after Add to Cart on the product page vs
      "not on the product page": the header cart is the product page's
      cart button, so it pulses and counts instead.
    · "Video review" is closed by default (the mockup shows it open), to
      keep the page short and the video from loading for everyone.
  - Section titles use sentence case like the mockup ("How to use",
    "Video review").
  - The header buttons stay round glass circles in both states; only the
    bar behind them fades in (cleaner than morphing the buttons).
  - Sticky bar uses the same quantity as the main stepper.
  - Glass look: the bento tiles in the code have no blur of their own,
    so I built the glass from the bottom nav's frosted look plus a light
    border and soft shadow (new tokens --glass-* in tokens.css, light and
    dark).


6. INDUSTRY-LEVEL SUGGESTIONS (not implemented)
-----------------------------------------------
1. Stock limit on the cart page (small). The cart page's + still lets a
   shopper go past the stock count; the order would then fail or be
   short. Same "Only N in stock" rule as the cards.
2. Reviews / star rating on the product page (medium). The page now has
   trust boxes and a video, but no customer reviews — the biggest trust
   signal on most shops.
3. Delivery estimate (small). "Fast delivery" is a claim; "Dhaka 1–2 days,
   outside 3–5 days" near Add to Cart is more convincing and cuts
   "when will it arrive?" messages.
4. Home speed (large) — unchanged from Batch 26: one 750 KB script.
   The shop script is now 753.7 KB (Batch 26 reported about 750 KB), so
   this batch added only a few KB.
