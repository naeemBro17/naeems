WHAT WAS BUILT (short)
====================================================================
1. Dashboard. For you (and anyone with "View profit & costs"), Admin → Home
   is now the Dashboard. At the top there are only four things:
   - Your profit for the period (big). Below it: ▲/▼ % against the period
     before, "You took ৳…" and "Left in the business ৳…".
   - Return on money in the business, with the same formula as the Money
     page.
   - Money now: Cash, bKash, Bank, With Steadfast, To receive, To give and
     Stock value, with the green "Books balance" badge (or a red "Off by
     ৳…").
   - Needs attention: one line per problem, and each line opens the right
     screen. There are new lines for:
     * products expiring before they sell;
     * sold without stock (still open);
     * customers owing for over 30 days;
     * products with no buy price;
     * products with no weight;
     * the monthly stock count when it is due.
     The lines you already had are still there (orders to confirm, low
     stock, dues, Steadfast not paid, reviews, wholesalers).
   - The period switch is Today / This month / This year.
   - On a computer (1024 px and wider) there is the left menu, and the
     cards sit side by side in 3 columns (the page is at most 1,240 px
     wide).
   Staff without "View profit & costs" keep the old Home exactly as it was.

2. Reports: a new "Reports" item in the menu, right after Money. It has 10
   reports. Each one has the dates Today / Yesterday / This week / This
   month / Last month / This year / All time / Custom, plus "Download
   Excel" and "Print / PDF".
   - Profit & loss: every step, from Sales down to Left in the business,
     with each expense category under Expenses. For more than one month
     (e.g. This year) there is one column per month plus Total. A locked
     month is marked "(locked)".
   - Sales (like Kaarbar): entries, total, received, unpaid; by source
     (Website / Manual — Facebook / WhatsApp / Phone …, from the order's
     source field); the list of orders (tap one to open it).
   - Products: units sold, sales, cost, profit, margin %. Tabs: Best
     sellers · Slow (no sale in 60 days) · Low margin (under 15%) · Not
     moving, stock left.
   - Lots: per lot, units bought / sold / on orders / removed / left; buy
     price, trip costs (weight and other bills), landed cost, sales, profit
     so far, margin, value of the stock left.
   - Stock: every lot row with units, expiry and value at landed cost. The
     "Expiring (90 days)" tab says "sells N a month → will (NOT) sell in
     time".
   - Expenses: by category with its share %, and by month.
   - Customers: who owes what, split into 0–30 / 31–60 / over 60 days;
     top customers by what they bought and by profit.
   - Day book: every money movement, per account, with the balance before,
     the running balance after each line, and the closing balance (like
     Kaarbar's Day Book and Cash in Hand).
   - Investors: capital in, returned, still owed, profit share paid (all
     time and in the chosen dates).
   - Charts: Your profit by month (bars, last 12 months) and Money in vs
     money out by month. Labels are on the bars, there is no 3D and nothing
     moves; the numbers are also in a table under the charts.
   The Excel file has one sheet per table. Amounts are real numbers with a
   ৳ format (Excel can add them up), and dates are real dates.

3. Monthly routine (Money → Monthly stock count, and Money → Lock a month;
   both are also at the bottom of Reports):
   - Count. Each month the system picks 15–20 products:
     * enough that every product with stock is counted at least once in
       about 6 months: first the ones never counted, then the ones counted
       longest ago;
     * the rest of the list goes to expensive stock and fast sellers not
       counted in the last 60 days, so they are counted more often.
     The screen shows "System says N" and you type what you counted (packs
     + loose units for products like fish oil in ml). When you save:
     * fewer than the system → a write-off "Count difference" at landed
       cost;
     * more → the extra units go back into the newest lot of that product.
     You can also count any other product any time ("Count another
     product"). Like Remove stock, saving works only while Inventory mode
     is On.
   - Lock. First a checklist:
     * the month is over;
     * the books balance;
     * no order from that month is still To confirm / Confirmed / In
       transit;
     * every Steadfast payout of that month is placed in an account;
     * the month's count is done.
     When every line is ticked, the Careful actions box has "Lock
     September 2026…", which asks for your password.
     After that, nothing dated in that month can be added, changed or
     deleted: orders, order items, payments, money entries, lots (and
     their bills), write-offs, cost adjustments and opening balances. The
     database itself refuses, with this message: "September 2026 is
     locked: … Add a correction dated today with the note 'correction for
     September 2026'."
   - Unlock: only the Super Admin, with the password and a written reason.
     The reason goes into the Activity Log, and so does every lock.

WHERE THE NUMBERS COME FROM (the brief's main rule)
====================================================================
Nothing works profit out a second way:
- Dashboard: exactly the Money page's database call (money_summary). The
  "period before" uses money_profit_parts (Batch 41).
- Profit & loss, its month columns and the charts: money_profit_parts for
  each date range (Batch 41), which is built on inventory_sales_profit
  (Batch 39).
- Expenses by category: the same expense rows money_profit_parts adds up.
- Products: inventory_sales_profit's own per-product table.
- Lots: each delivered line's sale is shared over its lots by units, with
  the same discount sharing as Batch 39. The test checks that the lots'
  sales and costs add up to Sales profit's totals.
- Stock: the same lot rows as the Money page's "Stock at landed cost".
- Customers: the same per-customer dues as Customers → To receive (Batch
  41).
- Day book: the same money ledger the account balances add up.
- Investors: the same rows as the Investors page.
The database test checks every one of these against the Batch 39 / 41
functions for the same dates (see TESTS).

The one report that answers a different question on purpose is Sales. It
counts orders on the day they were PLACED, like Kaarbar, while profit counts
an order on the day it is DELIVERED. The screen says this under the title.

CHOICES I MADE FOR YOU (and why)
====================================================================
1. The ▲/▼ % compares like with like: today vs yesterday; this month so far
   vs the same days of last month (1–10 Nov vs 1–10 Oct); this year so far
   vs the same days of last year. A whole last month would always look
   bigger in the middle of a month.
2. Home keeps your order tiles (Today / To confirm / With courier / This
   month) and the 7-day sales chart, under "Orders" BELOW the Dashboard.
   They are daily work, not profit, and removing them would have broken
   working features and their tests. The four Dashboard items are at the
   top as the brief asks.
3. "This week" starts on Saturday (the first working day in Bangladesh).
4. Counted MORE than the system: the extra units go back into the newest
   lot at its landed cost. They are recorded as a write-off row with a
   negative cost (marked "added back"), so "Stock removed (write-offs)" in
   profit is losses minus finds and the books stay balanced. I did not
   widen the old cost-adjustment reasons, because that would have meant
   dropping and re-creating a database rule.
5. How many to count: ceil(products with stock ÷ 6), at least 15, at most
   20. With more than 120 products in stock, 20 a month takes a little
   longer than 6 months to go round (150 products ≈ 7.5 months). The 20
   cap is from the brief.
6. A month whose count list was never made (the count screen was not
   opened in it, e.g. before Inventory mode) does not block its lock for
   ever: the line says "No count list was made that month." A list not
   finished by the end of the month can be finished early in the next one;
   those counts belong to the old list.
7. An order is "in" a month if it was placed in it, or became Delivered /
   Returned in it (the day its profit counts). After the lock only the
   things money depends on are refused (status, prices, discount,
   delivery, total, the date, items); a note or tracking number can still
   change. Today's sales can still take stock from an old lot; only the
   lot itself (what was bought, prices, bills, expiry) is locked.
8. Who can lock: anyone with "View profit & costs", with their password.
   Unlock: only the Super Admin, with the password and a reason.
9. Steadfast's own automatic records (courier fees, payout lines) are not
   refused by the lock, so the Steadfast sync can never break. The
   checklist line "Steadfast payouts recorded" makes sure they are placed
   before you lock.
10. Download Excel is the one orange button on every report. Print / PDF
    uses the phone's or computer's own print (Save as PDF); on paper the
    menus and buttons are hidden.

WHAT TO CHECK ON YOUR PHONE AND ON A PC (step by step)
====================================================================
Nothing below changes your books if you follow it exactly.
 1. Open Admin. Home is now the Dashboard. Look at Your profit, Return on
    money, Money now and Needs attention. (Until the opening balances are
    confirmed most numbers are ৳0 and the badge says "No check yet".)
 2. Tap Today, This month, This year. The numbers change and the ▲/▼ line
    says what it compares with.
 3. Tap a Needs attention line (e.g. "products with no weight"); it opens
    the right screen. Go Back.
 4. Menu → Reports. Open each of the 10 reports. Try the dates (This year
    on Profit & loss shows one column per month).
 5. On Profit & loss tap "Download Excel" and open the file. Check the
    Net profit total is the same as on the screen.
 6. Tap "Print / PDF" once and cancel (or Save as PDF).
 7. Money → Monthly stock count. Read the list and "System says N". While
    Inventory mode is Off it says so and saving is off. Once Inventory mode
    is On: count 2 products, type what you counted, tap Save; any
    difference shows in Money → Stock removed.
 8. Money → Lock a month. Pick last month and READ the checklist. Do NOT
    tap Lock.
 9. On a computer, open Home and two reports. The left menu shows, the
    Dashboard cards sit side by side, and the tables fit the page.
