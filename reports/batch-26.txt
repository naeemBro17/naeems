1. WHAT I DID
-------------

PART 1 — Brands in the database
  Before: a product's brand was plain text, and only 3 of 107 products had
  one filled in (Aveeno, Kirkland, "La Roche Posay").
  Now: a real brands table. Each product points to its brand by id.
  - The brand at the start of every product name was matched (capitals,
    spaces, dashes and accents ignored, so "CERAVE" = "CeraVe" and
    "La Roche Posay" = "La Roche-Posay"). Result: 44 brands, and ALL 107
    products matched. None left over.
  - Brand fields: name, link (e.g. la-roche-posay, unique, editable),
    logo, dark-mode logo, banner image, banner video (upload or YouTube),
    Show on home, display order, last updated by/at.
  - The old text column is kept and always kept in step, so nothing that
    reads it breaks (search, filters, CSV export, the live site).
  - Rename a brand and every product shows the new name straight away
    (proof: e2e test renames a brand and checks the products and the
    product page).
  - Typing a NEW brand name on a product (or in a CSV import) makes that
    brand automatically, with Show on home OFF. The product editor now
    suggests the existing brands as you type.

PART 2 — Admin → Brands
  In the CATALOG group, where Batch 25 reserved its place (phone: More →
  Brands). Same design as the other pages: rows on a phone, a table on a
  computer, ⋮ menus, line icons, no emoji.
  - Each brand: logo, name, number of products, link, "Show on home"
    switch, ⋮ (Edit · View on site · Delete).
  - Drag the dotted handle to reorder. That order is the Home row's order.
    A reorder is ONE Activity Log line ("Changed the order of brands").
  - Delete only works when no product uses the brand. Otherwise: "Move
    its products to another brand first." (the database refuses too).
  - Edit screen, with the help text you asked for under every field:
    Name, Link (shows the full link as you type), Logo, Logo for dark mode
    (optional), Banner image, Banner video (optional: "Upload video
    (recommended)" or "YouTube link"), Show on home.
  - Logos auto-fit: on upload, in the browser, empty transparent (or white,
    for a JPG) border is trimmed, it's resized to at most 800 px wide,
    transparency is kept, and it's compressed (WebP). A live preview shows
    the logo on a light card and on a dark card before you save.
  - Banner images are compressed like product photos. Videos are checked
    before upload: MP4/WebM only, 15 MB max, with a clear message showing
    the video's size if it's too big.
  - Security: only you and staff with the new "Edit brands" switch (OFF by
    default) can change brands, upload brand pictures or reorder. This is
    checked inside the database. Every add / change / rename / delete /
    reorder is in the Activity Log with who did it.

PART 3 — Home: "Shop by Brand"
  - Below the bento, above the category chips. "Shop by Brand" on the
    left, "See all →" on the right (grey, not orange — the cart buttons
    stay the one orange action).
  - One swipeable row, wide 2:1 cards, about 2.5 visible on a phone. Only
    the logo, centred; no logo yet = the brand name as neat text.
  - Light mode: white card + normal logo. Dark mode: dark card + white
    logo; without a white logo, a light card + normal logo.
  - Only brands with Show on home ON and at least one live product, in
    your order.
  - No layout jump: logo boxes have fixed sizes and logos load lazily;
    while the catalogue loads, the row's place is held (skeleton cards) if
    the last visit had a row.
  - The row uses the browser's own sideways scrolling (no touch code), so
    up/down page scrolling with a finger on the row works normally.

PART 4 — All Brands (/brands)
  "See all →" opens it: same cards, 2 per line, name and "12 products"
  under each. Header ← + "Brands", like the other inner pages. Back
  returns to the exact spot on Home. Brands with no live products aren't
  listed.

PART 5 — Brand page (/brand/<link>)
  - Banner (16:9): uploaded video plays muted, looping, inline, with the
    banner image as its poster; no sound button, controls or fullscreen.
    Reduce-motion or data-saver on → the picture only. No video → the
    picture. Neither → a soft grey gradient. A YouTube link plays the same
    way (muted, looping, no controls), with a cover over it so nothing can
    be tapped through to YouTube. YouTube still shows its title for a
    moment when it starts, so an uploaded MP4 is better.
  - Logo on a rounded plate over the banner (same light/dark rules).
  - Brand name (large) + "12 products".
  - Category chips: only the categories this brand has products in, "All"
    first; the choice is in the address, so Back brings it back. (Hidden
    when the brand has only one category — a single chip would do nothing.)
  - Product grid: the same product cards as Home (cart button, price,
    stock, options). Tap opens the product with the same picture
    animation; Back returns to the same spot. I didn't change any
    animation or scroll code; the brand page uses what's already there.
  - Unknown link → "Brand not found" + "See all brands".
  - Page title "<Brand> — Naeem's". Link previews (WhatsApp, Facebook ...)
    show "<Brand> — Naeem's", a one-line description and the banner (or
    logo), through a small server function like the product one.
  - The product page's small brand name is now a link to the brand page.
    Nothing else on the product page changed.
  - Brand pages are added to the sitemap for Google.

PART 7 — Three admin extras
  1. Restock: product ⋮ → "Restock" (right after Edit). A sheet with the
     current stock and two ways: "Add" (e.g. +24 arrived) or "Set to" (an
     exact count), one row per option for products with variants, and a
     preview "21 → 45". The database does the maths and saves it, writes
     "Restock: 21 → 45" (or "Restock (AU · 50ml): 3 → 7") to the Activity
     Log, and updates "Last updated by". Needs "Edit products and stock";
     the database refuses it otherwise.
  2. 7-day sales chart on admin Home, under the 4 cards: one bar per day
     (Bangladesh time), total ৳ of orders that aren't cancelled. Today's
     bar orange, the others grey; day labels; tap or hover a bar to see its
     ৳ and order count. Only for you or staff with "See sales figures" —
     the database refuses the numbers to anyone else. An all-zero week is
     a flat baseline. Works in dark and light.
  3. Customer notes and tags: Customers → tap a customer → a private Note
     and Tags (type a new tag or tap one already used; "vip" becomes "VIP"
     if VIP already exists). Tags show as chips on the Customers list, and
     a "tag" filter appears next to the sort. Reading needs "View
     customers"; changing needs the new "Edit customer notes" switch (OFF
     by default) — staff without it see the note read-only. Customers can
     never read their own notes or tags by any route (proved by a test
     that asks the database as the customer, and as a visitor). Every
     change is in the Activity Log.

5. DESIGN DECISIONS
-------------------
  - Brand row lives "with" the chips: if you ever reorder Home's sections,
    it stays directly above the chips.
  - "See all", the brand page and /brands use no orange: on these screens
    the orange stays on the cart buttons.
  - Brand page header is ← and the light/dark switch only (the big brand
    name is right under the banner, a title in the header would repeat it).
  - Logo plate on the banner overlaps the bottom-left corner, like an app
    store page.
  - Restock: one Add / Set-to switch for the whole sheet (simpler than
    choosing per row); empty rows are left alone.
  - Chart: a single grey/orange series, no legend (the title says what it
    is), value shown above the bars for the tapped day (today by default),
    plus the 7-day total in the corner.
  - New brand pictures always get a new file name, so a replaced logo is
    never stuck in a phone's cache.


6. INDUSTRY-LEVEL SUGGESTIONS (not implemented)
-----------------------------------------------
1. Home speed (large). Lighthouse mobile ≈ 30 before and after this batch.
   The main cost is one big JavaScript file (750 KB) and the bento's big
   photos. Splitting the code per page and serving smaller hero photos
   would make the first screen appear much sooner on a slow phone — that
   is where most visitors decide to stay or leave.
2. Brand logos (small, your time not code). Real logos (transparent PNG +
   white version) make the row and brand pages look official — a strong
   trust signal for "authentic imports".
3. Brand SEO text (small). A short paragraph per brand ("Authentic
   CeraVe imported from Australia...") on the brand page would help Google
   send "CeraVe price in Bangladesh" searches straight to it.
4. Restock history per product (small). The Activity Log has it; a
   "Stock history" list on the product would make stock questions quick.
5. Customer tags in Orders (small). Show a customer's tags (e.g. "Late
   payer") on their order screen, where you decide to confirm or not.


SHORT STEP-BY-STEP FOR NAEEM
----------------------------
1. Admin → Brands (phone: More → Brands).
2. Tap a brand (e.g. CeraVe) to open it.
3. Logo → Upload: a transparent PNG works best. It's trimmed and resized
   for you. Look at the two preview cards (light and dark).
4. Logo for dark mode → Upload a white version (optional).
5. Banner image → Upload a wide picture for the top of its page.
6. Banner video (optional) → "Upload video": a 5–20 second MP4, max 15 MB.
7. Switch "Show on home" ON → Save.
8. Back on the list, drag the dotted handle to put brands in the order you
   want on Home.
To let a staff member do this: Team → Roles → their role → "Edit brands".
