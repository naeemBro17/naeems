Version 1.39.0 · Branch feature/batch-39-inventory-sales (pushed; no PR opened)

INVENTORY MODE IS STILL OFF. Nothing about the live shop's checkout or

====================================================================
WHAT WAS BUILT (short)
====================================================================
- Units and pack size. Every product (or each size of a product) now has
  a "Stock unit" (pcs / ml / g) and a "Pack size" (default 1). You set
  them in the product editor, under the options. Example: fish oil bottle
  = pcs, 400. Shoppers never see this.
- Lots keep stock and cost PER UNIT. A lot row can be typed in packs or in
  loose units. The opening stock file has 3 new columns at the end:
  stock_unit and pack_size (grey, just for reading) and extra_units (loose
  units in an opened pack).
- Lot form clean-up: no currency or exchange rate any more (all prices in
  taka; old lots keep theirs). A missing weight no longer blocks saving —
  the weight is guessed from the price and marked "weight guessed from
  price". Suppliers are picked from a list with "+ Add new" (the
  suppliers you typed before were moved into the list).
- Sales take stock from lots (only when Inventory mode is On): earliest
  expiry first. Cancel, Return, order edits and order delete give the
  units back to the exact lot rows they came from. The shop's stock number
  then always equals the whole packs left in lots, so "Only N left" and
  "Sold out" keep working as before.
- "Returned" is now a real order status. On an order that was sent (In
  transit or Delivered) there is a new Careful action "Mark returned"
  (type the order number to confirm). Its goods go back to stock.
- Selling without stock (admin only): the website still refuses. In a
  manual order or an order edit you get "Not in stock — sell anyway?". If
  you say yes, the missing units are listed in Inventory → Sold without
  stock until the next lot (or the opening stock) fills them.
- Profit: on each order page a "Profit" box (items sold, cost of goods,
  delivery charged, courier + COD fee, profit and %). Inventory → Sales
  profit shows any dates (Today / This month / Custom): totals, every
  delivered or returned order, and a table per product sorted by profit.
- Inventory mode switch: Inventory → "Turn on Inventory mode (check
  first)" shows a check screen, then needs your password. Turning Off
  again is a Careful action with a clear warning.
- Small fixes: sitemap (no duplicates, alphabetical, no hidden or test
  products, not rewritten when nothing changed), the "Needs weight" note,
  the old Batch 24 delete test now uses the Careful actions steps.

====================================================================
CHOICES I MADE FOR YOU (and why)
====================================================================
1. Pack size is a whole number (1 to 1,000,000). Stock is counted in
   whole units (pcs, ml, g), so a pack must hold a whole number of them.
2. Loose-unit lines are priced PER UNIT (e.g. ৳8 a pc × 150 = ৳1,200),
   like a shop till. The invoice and the customer's page show one line:
   "Fish Oil 1000mg — 150 pcs", 1 × ৳1,200. No cost or lot ever shows.
3. While Inventory mode is Off, loose-unit lines don't change the shop's
   stock number (it counts whole packs; part of a pack can't be taken
   off it). The screen says so.
4. The website's stock number = whole packs left: an opened bottle with
   250 pcs left does not show as "1 in stock".
5. "Returned" is set by you (Careful action), not automatically from
   Steadfast. Steadfast's "cancelled" can still change; the button
   explains when Steadfast says the parcel is coming back. A returned
   order cannot be reopened (it is final), and its goods always go back
   to stock (damaged returns: Batch 40).
6. When you turn Inventory mode On, orders that are still Processing or
   Confirmed (their goods are still on your shelf, so they were counted in
   your opening stock) can take their stock from lots at that moment.
   This is a tick box on the check screen, ticked by default. Missing
   units go to "Sold without stock". Orders already In transit are left
   alone (their goods have left the shelf).
7. Late bills and filled shortfalls are "cost adjustments" counted on the
   day they happened (the bill's date, or the day the lot was saved). The
   order's own profit keeps the cost locked when the stock left. If units
   that had an adjustment come back (cancel / return), the adjustment is
   undone on that day.
8. Delivery collected = the delivery fee on the order. Courier + COD =
   what Steadfast kept (from the Batch 36 payouts). Until Steadfast pays
   out an order, that line says "not known yet" and counts as ৳0.
9. Profit for one order counts on the last time it became Delivered
   (or Returned). Orders delivered before Inventory mode show "No cost
   (before inventory)" and are left out of the totals.
10. Order history (which staff without the profit permission can see)
    gets no lot or cost details. The stock taken / given back is written
    to the Activity Log as ONE entry per order action.
11. Suppliers: a supplier can be removed only if no lot uses it.
12. A new product created while Inventory mode is On shows the stock you
    typed until its first lot; the website still can't oversell it (the
    lots refuse). The stock fields in the product editor are locked while
    On, with a note.
13. The Inventory mode switch in Settings now shows the state and opens
    the check screen; the actual On/Off is there, behind your password.

WHAT I SHOULD CHECK ON MY PHONE (step by step)
====================================================================
NOW (Inventory mode Off):
 1. Admin → Products → open any product → under the options: "Stock unit
    & pack size". Use a TEST product (hidden), set pcs and 400, tap Save
    pack size.
 2. Admin → More → Inventory → New lot. Supplier: pick "+ Add new", type
    a name, Add supplier. Search your test product: you see "Packs (400
    pcs)" / "Units (pcs)". Type 2 packs at ৳1,200: it says "= 800 pcs in
    stock". Add a weight cost of ৳100: "weight guessed from price"
    appears. Tap Create lot. On the lot page: Units 800, cost per pcs.
    (There is no currency or exchange rate any more.)
 3. Inventory → Sales profit: Today / This month / Custom work (orders
    delivered before Inventory mode show "No cost (before inventory)").
 4. Inventory → Sold without stock: empty for now.
 5. Inventory → "Turn on Inventory mode (check first)": read the check
    screen — opening stock, differences, "These will show Sold out", pack
    sizes. The Turn On button stays grey until L-000 is confirmed.
 6. Open any order that is In transit: at the bottom, Careful actions has
    "Mark returned". (Only use it on a real return.)

AFTER you upload your real opening stock and turn Inventory mode On
(these need the mode On; use your hidden test product and a test lot):
 7. New order (manual): add the test product, tap "Loose pcs", type 150,
    price ৳8 per pcs, save. If the test product is hidden, first make it
    active only for this test, or use any real product with pack size
    set.
 8. Open that order: the Profit box shows sale ৳1,200, cost (150 × the
    lot's cost per pcs), profit.
 9. Cancel the order (type its number): Inventory → your test lot →
    Units left goes back up by 150.
10. Make a manual order for MORE than the lot has: you get "Not in stock —
    sell anyway?". Tap Sell anyway. Inventory → Sold without stock shows
    the missing units. Cancel that order afterwards.
11. Inventory → Sales profit → Today.
Note: a lot that has had sales can't be deleted (its history must stay),
so a test lot used for these steps stays in your list with its units.

INDUSTRY-LEVEL SUGGESTIONS (not built)

1. Stock count day (medium): count the shelf, record differences as
   adjustments (damaged, expired, lost). Needed once sales use lots —
   real stock drifts quietly otherwise.

   offer before they expire. Earliest-expiry-first selling helps, but a
   list makes it visible.
3. Damaged returns (medium, planned for Batch 40): write off returned
   goods that can't be sold again, so profit and stock stay true.
4. Profit by month chart and by source (small): Facebook vs website vs
   WhatsApp — shows where your profit really comes from.
5. Low-margin warning on the manual order (small): show when a custom
   price is below cost (only for people with "View profit & costs").
